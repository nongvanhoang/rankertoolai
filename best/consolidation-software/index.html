Sage, MYOB, FreeAgent and QuickBooks Groups

Most consolidation tools grew up around Xero and QuickBooks, so the ledger list is where they differ most. If any company in your group runs Sage, Joiin is the only tool in this list that names a Sage connection on its pricing page. For MYOB groups in Australia and New Zealand, both Joiin and Fathom list MYOB. For FreeAgent users, mostly UK small businesses and contractors, Joiin and Fathom both list it too.

For QuickBooks Online, the standard Simple Start, Essentials and Plus plans don't produce consolidated reports across separate company files. QuickBooks Advanced can pull several companies into Excel through Spreadsheet Sync, but accounts only line up if the charts of accounts match exactly, and it doesn't handle eliminations or currency translation. A connected tool (Joiin, Fathom, Spotlight, LiveFlow) or a move to Intuit Enterprise Suite are the usual next steps.

Running Xero? We cover the options in detail in How to Consolidate Multiple Xero Organisations.

Multi-Currency and Group Reporting

If a subsidiary keeps its books in another currency, the consolidation tool has to translate its figures into the group's reporting currency before adding them up. That's where entry plans often stop. On Joiin, Core reports in a single currency, Pro adds multi-currency, and Max adds advanced FX so you can apply your own rates, which matters when auditors or group policy specify them. Fathom Pro includes multi-currency consolidations, and Spotlight Multi handles multiple currencies for multi-national reporting.

A full group pack usually needs four statements: a consolidated profit and loss, a consolidated balance sheet, a consolidated cash flow, and intercompany reconciliation so the eliminations tie out. Joiin includes P&L, balance sheet, cash flow and trial balance on every plan. Whichever tool you trial, test it with a month that has real intercompany transactions, because that's where manual spreadsheets usually break.

How to Choose

Start with three questions:

  • Does it connect to every ledger in the group? A tool that misses one entity's system sends you back to spreadsheets for that entity.
  • Which plan includes eliminations and multi-currency? The headline price is often for a plan that doesn't include them.
  • What does the price depend on? Per company (Joiin), a bundle of companies (Fathom Pro, Spotlight) or a quote (Syft, LiveFlow, Intuit). Price your actual entity count.

For a business group with a handful of entities, Joiin's per-company pricing with consolidation on every plan is usually the cheapest way to get real consolidated reports. For an accounting firm already reporting on dozens of clients, Fathom or Spotlight's bundles can work out cheaper per company, and their advisory features go further.

FAQ

What is the best consolidation software for small business groups?

For most small and mid-sized groups, Joiin is the most direct fit: consolidation features (COA mapping and eliminations) are on every plan, it connects to ten ledgers including Xero, QuickBooks, Sage and MYOB, and published pricing starts at $28/mo for one company billed annually. Fathom and Spotlight are stronger choices for accounting firms that want a broad advisory suite and are happy to pay for a bundle of companies.

Can Xero consolidate multiple organisations on its own?

No. Xero has no built-in consolidated reporting across organisations, and Xero has said on its product ideas forum that it isn't currently planned. Groups either combine exports in a spreadsheet or use a connected app such as Joiin, Fathom, Spotlight or Syft. See our guide to consolidating multiple Xero organisations.

Can I consolidate companies that use different accounting software?

Yes, if the tool supports every ledger in your group. Joiin lists ten connections, including Xero, QuickBooks, Sage, MYOB and FreeAgent, so a group with one company on Xero and another on Sage can consolidate without migrating. Fathom lists Xero, QuickBooks, MYOB and FreeAgent. Always check every entity's system before you subscribe.

Do I need multi-currency consolidation?

Only if a subsidiary keeps its books in a different currency. On Joiin, multi-currency starts on Pro ($34/mo for one company, annual) and Max adds your own FX rates. Fathom Pro and Spotlight Multi both include multi-currency.

How is consolidation software priced?

Mostly by company count. Joiin charges per connected company with unlimited users. Fathom Pro bundles 25 companies for $450/month, plus $17 per extra company. Spotlight's advisor plans are priced by number of organisations (from NZ$329/month for up to 10). Compare the price for your exact entity count, not the headline figure.

What does intercompany elimination mean?

When companies in the same group trade with each other, those sales, costs, loans and balances would be double-counted if you simply added the ledgers together. Eliminations remove them so the consolidated figures only show the group's dealings with the outside world.
