6 Best AI Website Builders in 2026: Lovable vs Bolt vs Framer vs v0

Quick Answer

Best all-round for non-coders: Lovable (free plan, Pro $25/mo with custom domains). Biggest free plan: Bolt.new (1M tokens a month). Best-looking marketing or portfolio site: Framer (from $10/mo). #1 · Best Overall for Non-Coders

1. Lovable

Describe a site or app in plain English, then refine it in chat

Free · Pro $25/mo
Free planCustom domains on ProApps, not only pages2 months free on yearly

Lovable turns a written prompt into a working web app or site and lets you keep editing it by chatting. The Free plan gives 5 build credits a day (up to 30 a month) plus 20 Cloud credits a month, and lets you publish on up to 5 lovable.app addresses. Pro is $25 a month and adds 100 monthly credits on top of the daily ones, plus custom domains. Business is $50 a month, and Enterprise is volume-priced with GitHub Enterprise and data-residency options. Yearly billing gives two months free.

Pros

  • The easiest start here for someone who has never written code
  • Builds full apps (logins, data, forms), not only static pages
  • Free plan is enough to see whether it fits before paying

Cons

  • Credits run out fast on big rebuilds; plan your prompts
  • Custom domain needs Pro
  • Less fine design control than Framer

#2 · Best Free Plan

2. Bolt.new

Prompt-to-app builder from StackBlitz with hosting included

Free · Pro $25/mo
1M free tokens/monthHosting includedUp to 28% off yearly

Bolt.new builds sites and apps from a prompt and shows you the code as it goes. The Free plan includes 1 million tokens a month (with a 300K daily limit), website hosting, a 10MB upload limit and up to 3 collaborators. Pro is $25 a month, starts at 10 million tokens a month with no daily limit, and adds custom domain support. Teams is $30 per member per month. Bolt says yearly billing saves up to 28%.

Pros

  • Good middle ground if you want to see and tweak code

Cons

  • Tokens are harder to budget than a simple message count
  • Custom domain needs Pro
  • The code view can feel busy for complete beginners

3. Framer

Visual website builder with AI credits, CMS and hosting

Free · from $10/mo
Design-firstFree custom domain on paid plans2× credits first month

Framer is a visual builder for landing pages, portfolios and company sites, with AI credits for generating and editing pages. Free includes 500 AI credits a month on a framer domain, 1GB bandwidth and up to 10 CMS collections; a custom domain needs a paid plan. Basic is $10 a month with 1,000 AI credits, 30 site pages, 2 CMS collections and a free custom domain. Pro is $30 a month with 3,000 credits, 150 pages and 10 CMS collections. Cons

  • Made for websites, not apps with logins and databases

#6 · Best DIY Route

6. Claude

General AI assistant that writes the full site code for you to host anywhere

Free · Pro $20/mo
You own every fileHost anywhereMore steps

Many of the "I built this site with one prompt" videos use a general assistant like Claude rather than a website builder. You describe the page or share a screenshot you like, Claude writes the HTML, CSS and JavaScript, and you preview it in the chat. You then download the files and put them on any host, including free static hosting. Claude has a free plan, and Claude Pro is $20 a month for higher limits. The trade-off is that publishing, domains and forms are your job.

Pros

  • No lock-in: the code is yours and runs on any host
  • Strong at design variations and animations from a reference
  • The same subscription covers writing, research and coding

Cons

  • You handle hosting, domain and forms yourself
  • No built-in database or login system like Lovable

Which One for Which Job

  • A site for your own small business: Framer if looks matter most, Hostinger if the lowest bill matters most.
  • An app with sign-ups, a database or payments: Lovable, or Bolt if you want the bigger free plan.
  • Websites for clients: build the first drafts in Lovable, Framer or Claude, then hand over on a host the client controls. Agree who pays for the plan and domain before you start.
  • You already write React: v0 or Bolt, and keep the code in your own repository.

Can You Really Build a Site in a Few Hours?

For the first version, yes. Any tool on this list can turn a prompt or a screenshot into an attractive, mobile-friendly draft in minutes. What still takes time is the part around the design: real text and photos, contact forms that actually deliver email, testing on a few phones, connecting the domain, basic SEO (titles, descriptions, page speed) and the edits that come after launch.

Be careful with videos that promise guaranteed clients or a fixed income from AI-built websites. Faster building lowers your cost; it doesn't create demand. If you plan to sell sites, show real examples, price the ongoing work (hosting, edits, support), and keep the client's domain and accounts in the client's name.

FAQ

What is the best AI website builder in 2026?

For most people who want a working site or app from a text prompt, Lovable is the best all-round choice: it has a free plan with 5 daily credits and Pro costs $25 a month with custom domains. Bolt.new has the most generous free plan (1 million tokens a month). For a polished marketing or portfolio site, Framer is the better fit from $10 a month.

Is there a free AI website builder?

Yes. Lovable, Bolt.new, Framer and v0 all have free plans. Bolt gives 1 million tokens a month (300K a day), Lovable gives 5 build credits a day (up to 30 a month), Framer gives 500 AI credits a month on a framer domain, and v0 gives $5 of one-time credits with a 7-message daily limit. A custom domain needs a paid plan on all of them.

Lovable vs Bolt: which is better?

Both start at $25 a month for Pro. Lovable is easier for non-coders who want a finished app with a guided flow. Bolt suits people who are comfortable looking at code, and its free plan is larger: 1 million tokens a month against Lovable's 5 credits a day.

Can I really build a client website with AI in a few hours?

You can get a good-looking first draft in minutes and a launch-ready site in hours, but the slow parts are still the same: real copy and photos from the client, testing on phones, contact forms, domain setup and later edits. AI makes the build faster; it does not find clients or replace that checklist.

What is the cheapest way to get an AI-built website online?

Hostinger's Premium plan includes its AI builder, hosting and a free domain for the first year from $1.99 a month, but that price needs a 48-month term and renews at $10.99. Framer Basic is $10 a month with a custom domain. If you can handle a few technical steps, Claude can write the code and you can host a simple site for free.
