How to Choose

Start with three questions:

  • Do you need hours or insight? If you only need accurate timesheets, Hubstaff, Time Doctor or DeskTime cover it cheaply. If you want to see who is overloaded and how work is spread across the team, Insightful and ActivTrak go further.
  • What will 10 or 50 seats really cost? Multiply the per-seat price by your headcount and add any add-ons you need (screenshots, recording, payroll). Annual prices are lower but lock you in.
  • How long must you keep data? Mera Work keeps about three months. If you review trends quarter over quarter or year over year, pick a tool with longer retention.

For most teams of 10 to 200 people, Insightful is the safest first trial: the trial has no employee limit and the free demo helps with setup. For a small team where every dollar counts, start with Mera Work and move up only if you outgrow it.

Monitoring Employees Fairly

Monitoring works best when people know about it. Tell your team what is tracked, why, and who can see it; track work devices and work hours only; and prefer aggregate reports (time per app, workload per person) over constant screenshots. In the UK and EU, GDPR expects a clear legitimate purpose and proportionate monitoring, and some US states require written notice. Check local rules before you roll out any tool on this list.

FAQ

What is the best employee monitoring software for a small business?

For teams of about 10 or more that want to see workload and capacity, Insightful is the strongest all-round choice at $8 per seat per month billed annually, with a 7-day trial and a free live demo. If price matters most, Mera Work costs $3 per user per month billed annually with every feature included. ActivTrak's free plan covers up to three users.

What is the cheapest employee monitoring software?

Among the tools here, Mera Work has the lowest paid price: $3 per user per month billed annually or $4 monthly, so 10 people cost about $30 a month. ActivTrak is free for up to three users, and Hubstaff Starter is $4.99 per seat.

Is there a good alternative to Hubstaff or Time Doctor?

Yes. Insightful adds capacity and workload analysis on top of time tracking from $8 per seat, and Mera Work is cheaper than both at $3 per user (annual). Hubstaff is still a good fit if you need payroll or GPS locations, and Time Doctor if you want attendance and month-to-month billing.

Is employee monitoring legal?

In most places it is legal to monitor company devices for work purposes, but rules on notice, consent and what you may record differ by country and state. Tell employees what is tracked and why, avoid tracking personal devices, and check local law (for example GDPR in the UK and EU) before you roll a tool out.

How much does employee monitoring software cost for 10 people?
