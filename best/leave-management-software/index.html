Leavo stands out because time tracking and shift planning are included in its entry plan, where most leave tools charge extra or do not offer them at all. Professional is $2.50 per user per month billed yearly ($450 a year for 15 members; $3 billed monthly) and Premium is $4.17 ($5 monthly), adding unlimited organizations, the maximum AI quota, extra AI credits, a dedicated account manager and priority support.

#2 · Simplest Holiday Booking

2. Timetastic

Leave and absence planner, with an optional rota add-on

From $1.50/user/mo
30-day trialMonthly, no contractMobile app

Timetastic is the cheapest tool here for plain holiday booking. Business is $1.50 per user per month and Pro is $2.50, paid monthly with no minimum term. Business covers holiday approvals, allowances, a shared calendar, locked dates, a mobile app and Slack and Google Workspace integrations. Pro adds leave accruals, annual booking limits per leave type, absence insights, a burnout board, company-wide absence reports, Microsoft Teams and SSO.

Shift planning and clock-in are a separate product, Rota & Time Clock at $5.50 per user per month. Timetastic offers a 50% discount for charities, B Corps and NHS Trusts. Prices were shown in US dollars when we checked; the company is UK-based.

Pros

  • Lowest entry price for leave only
  • Month-to-month, cancel any time
  • 50% discount for good causes

Cons

  • Accruals and booking limits need Pro
  • Shifts and timesheets cost $5.50/user
  • Microsoft Teams only on Pro

4. Calamari

Modular HR: time off, attendance, core HR, scheduling

From 2/user/mo (yearly)
Free trialModular pricing10-seat minimum

Calamari sells HR modules you can combine. The Time Off module is listed at 2 per employee per month billed yearly (2.5 monthly), and Calamari bills in EUR or USD. Time & Attendance, Core HR and Scheduling are separate modules at a similar per-user price. The minimum charge equals a 10-seat subscription.

The free trial includes help with setup and data import.

How to Choose

  • Only holidays and sick days? Timetastic Business is the cheapest and simplest.
  • Leave plus timesheets or shift rotas? Leavo covers all three in one plan.
  • Team runs on Slack or Teams? Vacation Tracker, if the yearly minimum fits your headcount.
  • Strict notice rules, multi-step approvals or several countries? Calamari.
  • Need payroll and hiring too? A full HR suite will cost far more per user; start with a focused tool unless you need those modules now.

Whichever you pick, set up your leave types and allowances during the trial and invite two or three managers to approve real requests before you commit.

FAQ

What is the best leave management software for a small business?

For leave plus time tracking and shift planning, Leavo is the best value here at $2.50 per user per month billed yearly, with a 30-day trial. For holiday booking only, Timetastic Business at $1.50 per user is the cheapest and simplest.

Is there free leave management software?

None of the four tools here has a permanent free plan. Leavo and Timetastic offer 30-day free trials, Vacation Tracker 14 days, and Calamari a free trial with setup help.

How much does leave management software cost for 15 people?

At entry prices: Timetastic about $22.50 a month, Calamari about 30 a month for Time Off, Leavo $37.50 a month billed yearly ($450 a year) and Vacation Tracker about $45.83 a month because of its $550 yearly minimum.

Which leave tool includes time tracking and shift planning?

Leavo includes time tracking, badge and clock devices and shift planning in its Professional plan. Timetastic sells Rota & Time Clock separately at $5.50 per user, and Calamari sells Time & Attendance and Scheduling as separate modules.

Does Leavo have a free trial?

Yes. Every Leavo plan starts with a 30-day free trial with full access, unlimited members and no credit card.
