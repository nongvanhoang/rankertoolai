What Our Own Tracking Showed

We've tracked RankerToolAI in LLM Pulse since October 28, 2026, using 60 prompts across five AI models. It's a single small site over two weekly runs, so treat it as an example, not a benchmark.

  • 0 mentions in 600 AI answers. Across the runs of late October and November 2, no assistant named RankerToolAI.
  • 1 citation per run. Both came from ChatGPT, for the same question, pointing to our Shopify tools page, and as a background source rather than a ranked link.
  • The big directories were rarely named either. The eight AI-tool directories we track drew just 12 mentions on non-brand prompts in the November 2 run.
  • Sources were the bigger story. In that run, YouTube appeared in 30% of answers, Reddit in 24%, Zapier in 14% and G2 in 9%.

The practical lesson for us: a tracker shows you who AI listens to, and that list may not be who you assumed your competitors were. It didn't raise our visibility on its own. Screenshots from our account are in our LLM Pulse review.

How to Choose a GEO Tool

  • Count your prompts first. Write the 20 to 50 questions your buyers really ask. If you need more than 15, Otterly's Lite plan won't cover it.
  • Check which engines are included. Add-ons can erase a low starting price, so compare the plan with the engines you actually need.
  • Be honest about frequency. A small site rarely needs daily data, because little changes day to day.
  • Plan what you'll do with the data. Without time to improve pages and earn mentions on the sources AI cites, any tracker just reports the same number every week.

FAQ

What is a GEO tool?

A GEO (generative engine optimization) tool asks AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity the questions your buyers ask, then records whether your brand is mentioned, which pages get cited, and how you compare with competitors.

What is the cheapest GEO tool?

Among the tools here, Otterly.AI has the lowest listed entry price at $29/mo, or $25/mo billed annually, for 15 prompts. LLM Pulse costs €49/mo but includes 50 prompts and five AI models, so it costs less per prompt.

Will a GEO tool get my brand mentioned by ChatGPT?

No. These tools measure visibility and suggest fixes, but you still have to do the work. After two weekly runs of our own tracking, RankerToolAI had 0 mentions in 600 answers and 1 citation per run.

Do I need daily tracking?

Probably not at first. AI answers vary from run to run, and a small site changes slowly, so weekly data is enough to spot which sources and competitors keep appearing. Daily tracking matters more for large brands running active campaigns.

Can I connect a GEO tool to Claude or ChatGPT?

LLM Pulse offers an MCP connection on every plan. We use it to pull our own tracking data into Claude. Check other vendors' current plan pages for their API or MCP availability.
