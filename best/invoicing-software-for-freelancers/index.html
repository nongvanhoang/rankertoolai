#1 · Best AI Invoicing

1. Billing Now

AI invoicing, receipt scanning and payment links

$9.99/mo or $99.90/yr
3-day trial, no cardUnlimited clients & usersOne planStripe payment links

Billing Now is invoicing software from Cappsule, a company in Geneva. There is one plan: $9.99 per month or $99.90 per year (about $8.33 a month), with unlimited invoices, expenses, clients and team users. That flat price is the main draw: FreshBooks limits clients by plan and charges per extra user.

You can type or say requests such as "create an invoice for John, consulting, $2,500" and the AI assistant drafts it for you to confirm. It also scans receipts into expenses, records payments, chases overdue invoices and exports P&L, tax summaries and invoice history to PDF, CSV or Excel. Invoices, estimates, credit notes and recurring invoices are included, plus Stripe payment links that mark the invoice paid when money arrives and Swiss QR bills. PayPal is listed as coming soon.

2. Zoho Invoice

Free invoicing from Zoho

Free
Free forever500 invoices/yr2 users

Zoho Invoice is free. The free plan allows up to two users (and two organizations), up to 500 invoices a year and three projects. It includes quotes, recurring invoices, automatic payment reminders, expenses, time tracking, a customer portal, reports and online payment options, plus 24/5 email and phone support. Invoices carry "Powered by Zoho Invoice" branding.

Pros

  • Genuinely free with no card
  • Recurring invoices, reminders and a client portal
  • Connects to Zoho Books and CRM if you grow

Cons

  • 500 invoices a year and two users
  • Zoho branding on invoices
  • No AI invoice assistant

Invoice Ninja's hosted Free plan covers 5 clients with unlimited invoicing, online payments, recurring invoices and auto-billing, deposits and partial payments, projects and time tracking, expenses and a client portal. 4. FreshBooks

Invoicing plus bookkeeping

From $28/mo
30-day trialClient limits by plan$11 per extra user

FreshBooks is the choice if you want invoicing and bookkeeping in one place. Regular prices are Lite $23 a month (5 billable clients), Plus $43 (50 clients) and Premium $70 (unlimited clients); Select is custom. Each extra team member costs $11 a month, and every plan has a 30-day trial. FreshBooks often runs introductory discounts, so check the price you will pay after the promotion ends.

Pros

  • Invoicing and accounting together
  • Mature apps and integrations
  • 30-day trial

Cons

  • Client limits on Lite and Plus
  • $11 per extra user adds up
  • Much more expensive than invoicing-only tools

How to Choose

  • Fewer than 500 invoices a year and a $0 budget: Zoho Invoice.
  • Five clients or fewer: Invoice Ninja Free.
  • Growing client list or a small team, flat price, AI help: Billing Now.
  • You also want bookkeeping and accountant access: FreshBooks.

Whichever you test, send yourself a real invoice and pay it with a card during the trial, so you see exactly what your clients will see.

FAQ

What is the best invoicing software for freelancers?

If you want a flat price with unlimited clients and an AI assistant, Billing Now at $9.99 a month or $99.90 a year. If you need it free, Zoho Invoice allows up to 500 invoices a year, and Invoice Ninja is free for up to 5 clients.

Is there completely free invoicing software?

Yes. Zoho Invoice is free for up to two users and 500 invoices a year, with Zoho branding on invoices. Invoice Ninja has a free hosted plan for up to 5 clients.

How much does Billing Now cost?

Billing Now has one plan: $9.99 per month or $99.90 per year, with unlimited invoices, expenses, clients and team users, and a 3-day free trial with no credit card.

Is FreshBooks worth it for freelancers?

FreshBooks is worth it if you want invoicing and bookkeeping together. If you only send invoices, it costs more than invoicing-only tools: Lite is $23 a month for 5 billable clients, plus $11 per extra user.

Can clients pay invoices online?

All four tools support online payments. Billing Now uses Stripe payment links (PayPal is listed as coming soon); Zoho Invoice, Invoice Ninja and FreshBooks support several payment gateways.
