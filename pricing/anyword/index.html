💡 The limit that matters is predictions, not words

Anyword doesn't cap how much copy you generate. What runs out is performance predictions: 50 a month on monthly Starter. If you mainly want the scoring, which is what sets Anyword apart from cheaper AI writers, the yearly plan doubles Starter to 100 predictions and lifts Data-Driven from 100 to 175. Which Anyword Plan Should You Pick?

Solo marketer or freelancer: Starter. You get unlimited copy, the Blog Wizard and one brand voice. Choose yearly if you'll use the scores every week.

Team of 2 to 3: Data-Driven. Three seats on the yearly plan work out to about $26 per person per month, cheaper than three Starter accounts ($117 a month billed yearly), and you get live scores while editing by hand.

Larger team with lots of ad data: Business. Its main extra is connecting your channels so Anyword learns from your past campaigns, plus website A/B testing. You'll need to talk to sales for a price.

Anyword Pricing — FAQs

How much does Anyword cost?

Anyword's Starter plan costs $49 a month, or $39 a month billed yearly ($468 a year). Data-Driven costs $99 a month, or $79 a month billed yearly ($948 a year). Business and Enterprise plans are custom-priced through Anyword's sales team. Prices checked on anyword.com/pricing on November 14, 2026.

Does Anyword have a free plan or free trial?

There is no free plan. Anyword offers a 7-day free trial on the Starter and Data-Driven plans, started from its pricing page.

Is Anyword's word count limited?

No. Every plan includes unlimited copy generation. What is limited is the number of performance predictions per month: 50 on Starter monthly (100 billed yearly) and 100 on Data-Driven monthly (175 billed yearly), according to Anyword's pricing page.

How much does an extra Anyword seat cost?

Starter includes 1 seat with no option to add more. Data-Driven includes 3 seats and lets you add seats at $59 a month, or $49 a month billed yearly, up to 10 seats.

How much do you save with Anyword's annual billing?

Annual billing cuts Starter from $49 to $39 a month and Data-Driven from $99 to $79 a month, about 20%. Over a year that is $120 saved on Starter and $240 on Data-Driven. The yearly plans also include more performance predictions.

Which Anyword plan should I choose?

Solo marketers and freelancers usually only need Starter. Pick Data-Driven if 2 or 3 people will share the account or you want live performance scores while you edit copy by hand. Business only makes sense if you want Anyword to learn from your own past campaign data (up to 5,000 data rows).
