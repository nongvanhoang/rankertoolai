Is Pro Unlimited?

No. Merlin's FAQ answers this directly: Pro gives "at least 30x the usage" of the free plan but is bound by fair-use limits, and those limits scale with the price you actually paid after any discount. If you hit them, Merlin sells top-up credits.

In practice: a deep discount (such as a Black Friday plan) buys you a smaller allowance than full-price Pro. If you plan to use the top models all day, check the fair-use terms before you pay for a year.

FAQ

Is Merlin AI free?

Yes. Merlin has a free plan with limited daily use of basic and Plus-level models, basic image generation and limited Chrome extension features. Our Merlin review recorded 102 requests a day.

How much is Merlin AI Pro?

$29 a month billed monthly, or $228 a year billed yearly, which works out to $19 a month (Merlin advertises yearly billing as saving 35%).

Is Merlin Pro unlimited?

No. Merlin's FAQ says Pro gives at least 30 times the usage of the free plan but is bound by fair-use limits, and you can buy top-up credits if you hit them.

Do I need a ChatGPT account to use Merlin?

No. Merlin's FAQ says you only need a free Merlin account.

Does Merlin AI give refunds?

The pricing page does not mention refunds. Our review noted a 3-day refund window on the Black Friday plan. Read Merlin's terms before you buy a yearly plan.
