AI Tool Pricing Index: November 2026

Published November 19, 2026 · By Hoang Nong · Data from review pages updated October 1, 2026 – November 18, 2026

What does it actually cost to start using an AI tool? We took the pricing tables from 99 AI and SaaS tools we review and put them side by side: who still offers a free plan, what the cheapest paid plan costs, and which categories are the most expensive to get into.

Key findings

have a free-forever plan (55 of 99)

The short version: most AI tools let you start for free, and more than half of paid plans start under $20 a month (47 of 87). The big exception is SEO software: only 3 of 11 SEO & content tools have a free plan, and the typical entry plan costs $99 a month.

What this means if you're buying

  • Start on the free plan. 56% of tools have one. Use it to check the output quality before paying.
  • Watch per-seat pricing. 19 tools charge per user. A three-person team on a per-seat plan pays three times the headline price.
  • Budget more for SEO. SEO and content platforms rarely have free plans. Use their trials on a real project before committing.
  • Annual billing changes the number. Where a vendor shows both, our entry price is the monthly rate when billed yearly. Paying month to month is often 15–40% more.

How we built this index

  • Source: the pricing table in each RankerToolAI review, which we take from the vendor's own pricing page. Only reviews updated between October 1, 2026 and November 18, 2026 are included (99 tools).
  • Free plan means a plan that costs $0 with no end date. A free trial that expires does not count.
  • Entry price is the lowest monthly price for one user on any paid plan, using the billed-yearly rate when the vendor shows it. Education-only discounts are excluded.
  • Left out of the price medians: 6 usage- or credit-based tools (no fixed monthly plan) and tools priced only in euros or Swiss francs. They still appear in the table and the free-plan figures.
  • Categories are our own grouping by what each tool mainly does.
  • Limits: prices change often. Vendors run promotions and test prices by region. Always check the vendor's page before you buy. See our review methodology.
