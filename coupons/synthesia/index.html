Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a Synthesia coupon code in 2026?

There is no public Synthesia coupon code. The best way to evaluate it risk-free is creating a free demo video, then choosing annual billing when you subscribe, which saves roughly 20% versus monthly.

Does Synthesia have a free plan?

Synthesia doesn't offer a permanent free plan, but new users can create a free demo video to test avatar realism and the editing workflow before subscribing.

How much does Synthesia's cheapest plan cost?

Starter costs $29/month for 10 minutes of video per month with 90 avatars, 1 user, and no SCORM export.

What's the difference between Starter and Creator plans?

Starter ($29/mo) gives 10 minutes of video, 1 user, and 90 avatars with no SCORM export. Creator ($89/mo) gives 30 minutes of video, 160+ avatars, custom backgrounds, and SCORM/LMS export for training platforms.

How much does Synthesia Enterprise cost?

Enterprise pricing is custom — unlimited video, team seats, a Personal Avatar (your own digital twin), SSO, and API access, priced based on team size and usage. Contact Synthesia sales for a quote.

How much can I save with annual billing?

Annual billing saves roughly 20% versus paying monthly. On the Creator plan, that brings the effective monthly cost down from $89/month to roughly $71/month equivalent.

Can I cancel my Synthesia subscription anytime?

Monthly plans can be cancelled at the end of the current billing cycle. Annual plans are billed upfront for the year — check Synthesia's current cancellation terms before committing to annual billing if you're not yet sure you'll use it long-term.

Is Synthesia worth it compared to HeyGen?

Synthesia's higher price is justified for enterprise and B2B training use cases needing SCORM/LMS export and 140+ language coverage. Individual creators on a budget are often better served by HeyGen's lower entry price. See our full HeyGen review.
