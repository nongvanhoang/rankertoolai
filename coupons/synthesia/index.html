Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a Synthesia coupon code in 2026?

There is no public Synthesia coupon code. The real savings come from the free Basic plan, which lets you test before paying, and from yearly billing, which saves about 38% on Starter and about 28% on Pro.

Does Synthesia have a free plan?

Yes. The Basic plan is free and includes about 10 minutes of video per month with 9 avatars. Videos are watermarked and cannot be downloaded, so it is mainly for testing.

How much does Synthesia's cheapest plan cost?

Starter is the cheapest paid plan at $29/month, or $18/month billed yearly. It gives about 12 minutes of video per month, 125+ avatars, downloads and no Synthesia logo.

What's the difference between Starter and Pro?

Starter ($29/mo, $18 yearly) gives about 12 minutes of video and 125+ avatars. Pro ($89/mo, $64 yearly) gives about 60 minutes, 180+ avatars, 5 personal avatars, interactive videos and branded pages. Neither includes SCORM export, which is Enterprise only.

How much does Synthesia Enterprise cost?

Enterprise pricing is custom. It adds unlimited video minutes, SCORM export, Brand Kits, 1-click translation, SSO and live collaboration. Contact Synthesia sales for a quote.

How much can I save with annual billing?

Yearly billing brings Starter from $29 to $18 per month (about 38% off) and Pro from $89 to $64 per month (about 28% off), as listed on synthesia.io in October 2026.

Can I cancel my Synthesia subscription anytime?

Monthly plans can be cancelled at the end of the current billing cycle. Annual plans are billed upfront for the year — check Synthesia's current cancellation terms before committing to annual billing if you're not yet sure you'll use it long-term.

Is Synthesia worth it compared to HeyGen?

Both let you start free. Synthesia is the stronger choice for company training thanks to 140+ languages and Enterprise SCORM export. Solo creators should compare both tools' current plans before paying. See our full HeyGen review.
