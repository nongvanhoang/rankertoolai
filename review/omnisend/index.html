Our Verdict

Omnisend is built specifically for ecommerce: email, SMS, and web push campaigns share one automation builder, and the platform plugs natively into Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, Wix, WordPress, Ecwid, and Shoplazza. The free plan is a genuinely usable starting point — 500 emails and 250 contacts with core automation and segmentation included, not a stripped-down trial — and Omnisend markets itself as up to 35% cheaper than Klaviyo at comparable list sizes. The catch is that both paid tiers are priced against contact-list size rather than a flat monthly fee, so the cost climbs as a store's list grows, and SMS is billed separately per message on top of the plan price. This verdict is built from Omnisend's published pricing, documented feature set, and independent third-party reviews — RankerToolAI has not run a hands-on lab test of Omnisend.

What Omnisend Offers

Email, SMS & Web Push in One Builder

Omnisend's automation workflows aren't email-only — the same visual builder triggers email, SMS, and web push notifications off the same events (cart abandonment, browse abandonment, purchase, welcome series), so a single automation can coordinate all three channels instead of requiring separate tools for each.

Native Ecommerce Platform Integrations

Omnisend connects directly to Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, Wix, WordPress, Ecwid, and Shoplazza, plus 200+ additional integrations via API. This native connection pulls in order data, product catalogs, and customer behavior automatically, which is what powers ecommerce-specific automations like abandoned cart recovery and post-purchase upsell flows without manual data syncing.

Omnisend AI

Omnisend AI assists with email and SMS copywriting from a prompt, recommends optimal send times per subscriber based on engagement history, and can auto-generate audience segments. It's built into the campaign editor as an assistive layer rather than a standalone generator — useful for drafting faster, not for hands-off campaign creation.

Forms, Segmentation & Reporting

Customizable, no-code signup forms and popups capture contacts directly into segments based on behavior and purchase history. Reports AI (Standard plan and up) and revenue-attribution reporting show which campaigns and automations are actually driving sales, not just opens and clicks.

Free Migration Service

Omnisend offers a free 5-day migration service for stores switching from another platform (commonly Klaviyo or Mailchimp), moving contacts, segments, and historical data over without a manual export/import process — a meaningful reduction in switching friction for an established store.

Best Omnisend Alternatives

Omnisend isn't the only option for ecommerce email and SMS marketing. Here's how the leading alternatives compare:

  • Klaviyo — The closest ecommerce-native competitor, with deeper analytics and predictive segmentation, but typically pricier at the same list size — Omnisend positions itself as up to 35% cheaper.
  • Mailchimp — A broader, general-purpose integration ecosystem, but less built around ecommerce-specific automations like abandoned cart and post-purchase flows.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Omnisend worth it?

For an ecommerce store on Shopify, WooCommerce, or BigCommerce, yes. Omnisend combines email, SMS, and web push in one automation builder with native store integrations, and the free plan is genuinely usable rather than a stripped-down trial. The tradeoff is that both paid tiers are priced against contact-list size, so cost rises as your list grows, and SMS is billed per message on top of the plan price.

Does Omnisend have a free plan?

Yes — Omnisend's Free plan includes 500 monthly emails, 250 contacts, and 500 web push notifications per month, with core features like forms, automation, and segmentation included rather than gated behind a paywall. It does not expire; it's a permanent free tier, not a time-limited trial.

What is the difference between Omnisend's plans?

Free ($0/mo) covers 500 emails and 250 contacts with core features included. Standard (from ~$11.20/mo at 500 contacts) raises the email cap to 6,000/month, adds unlimited web push, Forms AI, and Reports AI. Pro (from ~$41.30/mo at 2,500 contacts) unlocks unlimited email sends, AI-powered personalization, and priority support, with SMS billed separately from about $0.007 per message. A Custom/Enterprise tier is quote-based for larger lists and adds a dedicated account manager.

Does Omnisend's AI actually generate campaigns?

Omnisend AI assists with email and SMS copywriting, recommends optimal send times per subscriber, and can auto-generate audience segments. It's an assistive layer built into the campaign editor rather than a standalone generator app, and it's positioned as helping you draft and optimize campaigns faster rather than fully automating content creation.

What are the best Omnisend alternatives?

Klaviyo is the closest ecommerce-native competitor with deeper analytics and predictive segmentation, but Omnisend markets itself as up to 35% cheaper at comparable list sizes. Mailchimp offers a broader general-purpose integration ecosystem but is less built around ecommerce-specific flows like abandoned cart and post-purchase automation. GetResponse bundles funnels, webinars, and a course/site builder, making it a better fit for creators than pure ecommerce marketing.

Is Omnisend good for small businesses or solo creators?

For a small ecommerce store, yes — the free plan and Standard tier cover the core email/SMS/automation needs most small stores have without a big upfront cost. For a solo creator or business that isn't ecommerce-based, Omnisend's ecommerce-specific automations (abandoned cart, product recommendations, post-purchase flows) go mostly unused, and a more general tool like GetResponse or Mailchimp may be a better fit.
