Our Verdict

WriteHuman rewrites AI-generated drafts so they read more naturally, and bundles an AI text detector and an AI image detector in every plan. Limits are set by requests and words per request: Free gives 3 requests a month at up to 250 words, Basic 80 requests at 600 words, Pro 200 requests at 1,200 words with three output variations, and Ultra unlimited requests at 3,000 words. Billed yearly, Basic is $12/mo, Pro $19/mo and Ultra $39/mo; month to month they cost $20, $29 and $59. Use it to smooth stiff AI drafts for marketing copy, emails and blog posts you've checked and stand behind. Don't use it to pass off AI work as your own where that's against the rules, such as school or university assignments: no humanizer can guarantee a result against every detector, and getting caught costs far more than the subscription. The trade-offs: the free plan is only a taste, the 600-word cap on Basic means splitting long articles, and the site doesn't spell out its refund policy. This verdict is built from WriteHuman's own published pages — RankerToolAI has not run a hands-on lab test of WriteHuman.
