Our Verdict

LLM Pulse answers a question classic rank trackers can't: when someone asks ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini or Google's AI Overviews what to buy, does your brand come up — and who gets mentioned instead? It runs your chosen prompts against 5 AI models on a schedule and records mention rate, a position-weighted AI Visibility Score, share of voice against named competitors, and which URLs the models cite. Two details make it more trustworthy than most tools in this young category: prompts that already name your brand are excluded from competitor comparisons (so the numbers aren't inflated), and every metric links back to the actual AI answer it came from. The catch is price — €49/mo buys only 50 weekly-tracked prompts for one brand, daily tracking costs more (from €79/mo), and sentiment analysis needs the €99 Growth plan. This verdict is built from LLM Pulse's own published pricing and feature pages plus its public Trustpilot profile — RankerToolAI has not run a hands-on lab test of LLM Pulse.

What LLM Pulse Offers

AI Visibility Score

Measures how often AI answers mention your brand, both as a raw mention rate and as a score weighted by where in the answer you appear — being named first counts for more than a passing mention at the end.

Share of Voice vs. Competitors

Runs the same prompts for named competitors and shows who gets recommended more often, with brand-naming prompts filtered out so the comparison is fair.

Citations & Sources

Shows which URLs and domains each AI model cites when it answers, and which of those pages mention you — effectively a target list for PR and link building.

Sentiment Analysis (Growth+)

Checks how AI answers describe your brand, not just whether they name it — useful for catching outdated or negative framing.

Recommendations & GEO Writer

Suggests what to change to win more AI answers, derived from your own tracking data rather than generic advice, plus a writer to draft the content.

API, MCP Server & Looker Studio

REST API and an MCP server on every plan's data; full API, CLI and Looker Studio connector from the Scale plan for agencies building client reports.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is LLM Pulse worth it?

If AI assistants are becoming a real source of leads in your market and you want hard numbers on whether ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI answers recommend you, LLM Pulse is one of the more careful tools in this category. For a single brand, the €49/mo Starter plan is enough to start; agencies will need Scale for more projects and client reporting.

Does LLM Pulse have a free trial?

Yes — eligible new customers get a 14-day free trial on the weekly Starter, Growth and Scale plans. LLM Pulse also offers free tools such as an Agent Readiness Checker and AI text cleaners.

Which AI models does LLM Pulse track?

Every plan includes ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode and Gemini. Claude, Copilot, Grok, DeepSeek, Naver, Baidu and Meta AI are paid add-ons.

How much does LLM Pulse cost?

Weekly tracking starts at €49/mo (Starter), €99/mo (Growth) and €299/mo (Scale), excluding VAT. Daily tracking starts at €79/mo. Annual billing gives 2 months free.

What is GEO (generative engine optimization)?

GEO is the practice of improving how often and how favorably AI assistants like ChatGPT and Gemini mention your brand in their answers — the AI-search equivalent of SEO. Tools like LLM Pulse measure it so you can see whether your efforts are working. Our GEO guide walks through the full process.
