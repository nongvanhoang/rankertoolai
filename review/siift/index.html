What is siift?

siift is a web-based AI tool for one job: helping founders decide what to build and what to do next. The company describes it as an "execution filter" and an agentic AI operating system for founders and early-stage entrepreneurs. In plain terms, you describe your business or idea, siift keeps a structured picture of it, and the AI challenges your assumptions, backs its feedback with evidence and suggests the next concrete step.

That focus is what separates it from a general chatbot. A general assistant answers whatever you ask. siift is built around a fixed founder journey, so the conversation is anchored to your business canvas and to the stage you are in. Whether that structure suits you depends on how much guidance you want: first-time founders will probably value the scaffolding more than serial entrepreneurs who already have their own process.

siift is made by siift ai inc, based in Vancouver, Canada. Its website says it is used by "1,200+ serious builders" and displays logos of programs including AWS Startups, NVIDIA Inception and Founder University. These are vendor claims; we have not independently verified the user count or the nature of those program relationships.

The Five Stages: Ideate to Scale

siift organises its guidance around five stages. The stage names below are siift's; the descriptions are our reading of what each stage means for a founder.

  • 1. Ideate
  • 2. Validate
  • 3. Build
  • 4. Go To Market
  • 5. Scale
  • Ideate: shape a raw idea into something specific enough to test.
  • Validate: pressure-test assumptions before you commit time and money. This is where evidence-backed feedback matters most.
  • Build: decide what to build first and in what order.
  • Go To Market: plan how you reach your first customers.
  • Scale: work out what to change once something is working.

In our reading, the weight of the product sits in the first two stages. If you are already past product-market fit and running a team, much of what siift offers is likely to overlap with processes you already have.

Key Features

siift says every plan, including Free, includes the full feature set. The features below are therefore available whichever tier you choose.

1. Intelligent Business Canvas

The canvas is the structured core of a siift project: one place that holds your business model as it develops. Because the AI's feedback and guidance work from this canvas, the aim is answers grounded in your specific business rather than generic startup advice. Every plan includes unlimited projects, so you can keep a separate canvas for each idea you are comparing instead of overwriting one.

2. Living Memory System (agentic memory)

siift's Living Memory System is its name for persistent, agentic memory: the tool keeps track of your project context over time, so you don't have to re-explain your market, customer and decisions every session. For a founder working on an idea over weeks or months, that continuity is the main practical difference from starting a fresh chat each time.

3. Instant evidence-backed feedback and validation

siift positions its feedback as evidence-backed: when you propose an assumption, it responds with reasons rather than encouragement. This is the "execution filter" idea in practice, helping you drop weak ideas early. We haven't measured the quality or accuracy of that evidence, so treat it as input to your own customer research, not a substitute for talking to customers.

4. Proactive step-by-step guidance

Rather than waiting for you to ask the right question, siift suggests the next step based on your stage and canvas. This is most useful for first-time founders who don't yet know what they should be checking at each stage.

5. Human-in-the-loop automations across 80+ apps

siift says it can run automations across 80+ popular applications with a human in the loop, meaning you approve actions rather than letting the AI act unchecked. The product information we reviewed doesn't list which applications are supported, so if a specific tool in your stack matters, confirm it on the Free plan before you upgrade.

6. Shareable workspaces and views

Workspaces and views can be shared, which lets co-founders, advisers or early team members see the same canvas. Paid pricing is per user, so budget accordingly if several people need their own credits.

How siift Credits Work

siift meters usage in credits. The Free plan gives each user 50 credits a month, which siift says covers up to 50 AI requests depending on complexity. In other words, simple requests stretch the allowance furthest and more complex requests use it up faster. The pricing information we reviewed doesn't publish a per-request credit cost, so you can't calculate in advance exactly how many requests 200 or 1,000 credits will buy.

That makes the Free plan useful as a measuring tool, not just a trial. Because Free includes every feature, you can run a real project for a month, see how quickly your typical work uses credits, and then pick the tier that matches. If 50 credits lasts most of the month, Discover's 200 is likely enough; if you burn through 50 in a few sessions, Focus's 1,000 is the safer choice.

On Enterprise, credits are pooled instead of fixed per user, which suits organisations where a few people use the tool heavily and the rest rarely.

What siift Doesn't Do

siift's own FAQ says it does not help with HR, legal, compliance or finance. That is an honest boundary, and an important one for founders: company formation, contracts, hiring, regulatory questions and financial modelling all sit outside its scope. You will need separate tools or advisers for those. We'd rather see a tool state its limits than imply it covers everything, but it does mean siift is one part of a founder's stack, not all of it.

Who Should Use siift?

Based on its published features and pricing, siift fits these users best:

  • First-time founders at the idea or validation stage. The step-by-step guidance and evidence-backed feedback are most valuable when you don't yet know what to test or in what order.
  • Solo founders and side-project builders comparing several ideas. Unlimited projects and a full-featured Free plan let you keep a separate canvas for each idea at no cost.
  • Small founding teams. Shareable workspaces put co-founders and advisers on the same canvas, and Discover is $11/user/mo billed annually.
  • Founders in accelerator or incubator programs who want a structured place to keep their business model and decisions as they evolve.

Who should skip it: founders whose immediate need is legal, HR, compliance or financial work, which siift doesn't cover; established businesses that already have a strategy process and mainly need execution tools for sales, marketing or content; and anyone who wants a single, general-purpose AI assistant for every task rather than a dedicated strategy tool.
