Our Verdict

Clipto is an AI transcription tool that converts audio and video into text with speaker identification, timestamps, and translation into 99+ languages, plus an AI chat feature for querying transcripts directly instead of re-reading them. Its real Trustpilot rating (4.7/5) and a #1 Product of the Day finish on Product Hunt are strong independent signals of user satisfaction. Who Should Use Clipto?

Clipto's core value proposition is turning spoken content — meetings, interviews, lectures, podcasts, or social clips — into searchable, editable text without manual note-taking. It's aimed at anyone who regularly deals with recorded audio or video and needs an accurate written record fast.

Best fit for:

  • Multilingual teams and creators — 99+ language transcription and translation covers non-English source content that many transcription tools skip
  • Journalists and researchers — interview transcription with speaker labels, at a fraction of the cost of a professional transcription service
  • Content creators and marketers — export straight to SRT/VTT for subtitling YouTube, Instagram, or TikTok content
  • Students and lecture-note takers — long-form lecture and podcast transcription, with AI chat to quickly find a specific point discussed
  • Anyone who wants to query, not just read, a transcript — the AI chat feature is a genuine time-saver for pulling one detail out of a long recording

Consider alternatives if you:

  • Need a permanently free plan for light, ongoing use — Otter.ai's free tier (300 min/month) has no trial expiration
  • Live in Zoom/Google Meet/Teams and want a bot that auto-joins and transcribes meetings live — that's Otter.ai's specific strength
  • Need a full video/podcast editing suite built around the transcript (cut by editing text) — Descript is purpose-built for that workflow

What Clipto Offers

AI Transcription with Speaker ID and Timestamps

Clipto converts uploaded files, direct recordings, or online links into text transcripts, tagging who said what and when. This is the baseline feature most transcription tools offer, but it's the foundation everything else (summaries, translation, AI chat) builds on.

Translation Across 99+ Languages

Beyond transcribing in the source language, Clipto translates transcripts into any of 99+ supported languages — a meaningfully broader range than most competing tools, and useful for teams working across regions or content creators localizing videos for international audiences.

AI Chat Over Your Transcripts

Rather than scrolling through a long transcript to find one detail, Clipto's AI chat lets you ask a direct question ("what was the deadline discussed in this call?") and get an answer pulled from the transcript. This mirrors a feature Otter.ai users also cite as a genuine time-saver.

AI Summaries and Export Flexibility

Clipto generates AI summaries extracting key points and action items, and exports transcripts to TXT, DOCX, PDF, SRT, or VTT — covering plain documents, editable Word files, and subtitle formats for video platforms in one tool.

Best Clipto Alternatives

Clipto isn't the only AI transcription tool. Here's how the leading alternatives compare:

  • Otter.ai — Built specifically for live meetings, with OtterPilot auto-joining Zoom, Google Meet, and Teams. Has a genuinely usable free plan (300 min/month) that Clipto doesn't match, but Pro pricing ($16.99/mo) is higher and language coverage is narrower.
  • Descript — Pairs transcription with a full video/podcast editing suite where you edit the recording by editing the text. Better fit if editing is the end goal, not just a written record.
  • Rev.com — Offers both AI and human transcription, with human transcription priced per-minute and typically more accurate on difficult audio (heavy accents, poor audio quality, overlapping speakers) than any AI-only tool, at a higher cost.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Clipto free?

Clipto offers a 7-day free trial with full access to its premium features. After the trial, plans start at $9.99/month (billed monthly) or $12.49/month (billed annually, roughly 50% off the standard monthly rate). There is no permanent free tier for ongoing use beyond the trial period.

How accurate is Clipto's transcription?

Clipto advertises 99% transcription accuracy on its marketing pages. RankerToolAI has not independently benchmarked this figure against real audio samples — treat it as the vendor's own claim rather than a verified, third-party-tested number, and expect accuracy to vary with audio quality, accents, and background noise as it does with any AI transcription tool.

What languages does Clipto support?

Clipto supports transcription and translation across 99+ languages, making it usable for multilingual teams, international interviews, and non-English source content that needs an English (or other-language) transcript.

How does Clipto compare to Otter.ai and Descript?

Otter.ai is built specifically around live meetings (Zoom, Google Meet, Teams auto-join) and offers a genuinely usable free plan (300 min/month). Descript pairs transcription with a full video/podcast editing suite. Clipto's differentiators are broader language coverage (99+ languages) for translation, a lower entry price ($9.99/mo vs. Otter Pro's $16.99/mo), and an AI chat feature for querying transcripts — but it has no ongoing free tier, only a 7-day trial.
