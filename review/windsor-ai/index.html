Our Verdict

Windsor.ai pulls data from 350+ marketing, advertising, analytics and e-commerce platforms and sends it to BI tools, spreadsheets, warehouses and AI chats, with no code. On paper it is a strong fit for a small agency or PPC team that spends too long exporting reports, because the entry price is low and Claude and ChatGPT access through Windsor MCP is included on every plan. The catch is the plan structure: Basic allows only 3 data sources, and the next step costs about five times as much. Count your sources before you buy, and use the 30-day trial with your own accounts first.

What Windsor.ai Offers

350+ Data Connectors

Pulls data from platforms such as Google Ads, Facebook Ads, LinkedIn Ads, GA4, Shopify and Amazon. All connectors are available on all plans.

Destinations for Reports and Warehouses

Sends data to Looker Studio (Data Studio), Power BI, Tableau, Google Sheets, Excel, BigQuery, Snowflake, Redshift, Databricks, PostgreSQL, MySQL and more.

Windsor MCP for AI Chats

Connects your data to Claude and ChatGPT, and also works with Copilot, Gemini, Perplexity and Cursor, so you can ask questions in plain language instead of building a report first.

Scheduled Syncs

Destination tasks refresh your tables on a schedule: daily on Basic, daily or hourly from Standard, and every 15 minutes on Professional.

Client Access for Agencies

You can send an authorization link so a client connects a data source without sharing a password. Professional adds auto-add for all accounts within a source.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Windsor.ai free?

Yes. You get a 30-day free trial with 10 data sources, 15 accounts and 30 days of history. After that you can stay on the Forever Free plan with 1 data source and 1 account, or upgrade.

How much does Windsor.ai cost?

Billed yearly, Basic is $19 a month, Standard $99, Plus $249 and Professional $499. Billed monthly, they are $23, $118, $299 and $598. Enterprise is priced by quote.

Does Windsor.ai work with Claude and ChatGPT?

Yes. Windsor MCP is included on every plan and connects your data to Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot and other AI chats, so you can ask questions about your marketing data in plain language.

What counts as a data source?

A data source is the platform you pull data from, such as Google Ads or GA4. Facebook Ads, Facebook Pages and Instagram each count as one data source. Destinations such as Claude or Looker Studio do not count.

Can Windsor.ai change my campaigns?

Windsor.ai says read and write functions, including pausing campaigns and adjusting budgets, are available on every plan. Check permissions carefully before you allow an AI assistant to make changes.

Who is Windsor.ai best for?

Marketing agencies, PPC teams, analysts and e-commerce brands that report across several ad and analytics platforms and do not want to maintain their own scripts.
