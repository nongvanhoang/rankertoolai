Our Verdict

Alli AI is built for agencies and in-house teams that need to push SEO changes across many pages and sites without waiting for developers. You install it with a WordPress plugin or another integration — its site lists Shopify, Webflow, HubSpot, Salesforce, Cloudflare, Nginx, Apache, Akamai and AWS CloudFront, among others — then write rules that set titles, schema such as Product and Offer markup, internal links and other on-page elements on every matching page at once. It also serves clean, pre-rendered versions of your pages to more than 50 AI crawlers and agents, including those from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Perplexity and Microsoft, which matters most for JavaScript-heavy sites that bots otherwise see as blank. Business costs $249/mo billed annually ($299 monthly) for 5 sites, 500 keywords and 1,250 pages; Agency is $499/mo annually ($599 monthly) for 15 sites and 5,000 pages. Every plan has a 10-day free trial. The trade-offs: it's expensive unless you spread the cost across several sites, the page caps are tight for large stores, and changes applied through Alli's layer rather than your CMS need a plan for what happens if you cancel. This verdict is built from Alli AI's own published pages — RankerToolAI has not run a hands-on test of Alli AI.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Alli AI have a free trial?

Yes. Every plan includes a 10-day free trial. There's no free plan.

How much does Alli AI cost?

Business is $249/mo billed annually or $299 monthly, and Agency is $499/mo billed annually or $599 monthly. Enterprise is priced on request.

Does Alli AI work with Shopify and Webflow?

Yes. Its site lists Shopify, Webflow, WordPress, HubSpot, Salesforce and several server and CDN integrations.

Does Alli AI track ChatGPT mentions?

Not according to its site. It deploys optimizations and serves pages to AI crawlers; tracking mentions in AI answers needs a GEO tool such as LLM Pulse.
