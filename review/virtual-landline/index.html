Our Verdict

Virtual Landline gives a sole trader or small business a UK landline-style number that rings on a mobile or in an app, without installing a phone line. You can pick a local 01 or 02 number, a London number, a 0330 national number or a 0800 Freephone number, or port one you already own. Single-user plans run on a monthly rolling contract. Starter is £4.50 a month including VAT, with unlimited inbound calls in the app and no included outbound minutes. Basic is £6.50 and is an inbound service that diverts calls to your phone. UK Unlimited at £8.75 adds outbound calling, and Pro Unlimited is £10.95. The 0800 plan is £11.95 with 300 minutes. Read the word unlimited with care: the fair usage policy describes an allowance equal to unlimited UK landline calls or 1,000 UK mobile minutes a month, or a mix, and regularly making more than 40 calls a day is outside normal use. Starter excludes international and 084/087 calls and overseas diverts, and 0207/0208 London numbers cost more, from £12.50 a month. Teams that need a phone menu or call recording should look at the separate Virtual Office plans: call recording and menus are listed on Office Complete at £9.95 per user, with a two-user minimum. Trustpilot showed 4.6 out of 5 from 644 reviews on November 25, 2026; the negative reviews mention slow support and refused refunds. It is a UK service and not an AI receptionist: you or a colleague still answer the calls. For one person who wants a professional UK number, UK Unlimited looks like the sensible plan. This verdict is built from published pages — RankerToolAI has not run a hands-on lab test of Virtual Landline.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Virtual Landline cost?

Single-user plans are £4.50, £6.50, £8.75 and £10.95 a month including VAT. The 0800 Freephone plan is £11.95.

Is there a contract?

Single-user plans are listed as monthly rolling contracts.

Can I keep my existing number?

Yes, you can port an existing 01, 02, 03 or 0800 number.

Is calling really unlimited?

It is subject to fair use. The policy describes unlimited UK landline calls or 1,000 UK mobile minutes a month, or a mix, and more than 40 calls a day regularly is outside normal use.

Can a team use Virtual Landline?

Yes. Virtual Office plans start from £6.95 per user per month plus VAT with a minimum of two users.
