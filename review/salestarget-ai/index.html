Our Verdict

SalesTarget.ai puts lead discovery, cold email, LinkedIn automation, a CRM and a dialer in one platform, with an AI assistant called Copilot to help write campaigns. Lead Explorer finds, enriches and verifies contacts, email outreach includes warm-up and A/Z testing with a unified inbox, and the CRM covers deals, tasks and invoices. If you only need cold email, the Email Outreach plans start at $30/mo (10,000 emails), $79/mo (100,000 emails) and $299/mo (500,000 emails), and new customers get 30% off the first month on any monthly Email Outreach plan. The All-in-One plans add lead credits and LinkedIn: they start at $169/mo (Growth: 10,000 emails, 2,000 lead credits, 1 LinkedIn account, 1 user), Scale is $319/mo for 100,000 emails, 4,500 lead credits and 3 users, and Ultimate is $639/mo for 500,000 emails and 7,000 lead credits, with client workspaces and a dedicated account manager. You can also buy the CRM ($40/mo) or the dialer ($29/mo) on its own. The free trial gives 50 lead credits with no card. The trade-offs: Growth lacks the API, integrations and MCP server, extra LinkedIn accounts and users cost more, and current billing cycles aren't refunded. This verdict is built from SalesTarget.ai's own published pages — RankerToolAI has not run a hands-on lab test of SalesTarget.ai.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is SalesTarget.ai free?

There is a free trial with 50 Lead Explorer credits and no credit card. There is no permanent free plan.

How much does SalesTarget.ai cost?

Email-only plans cost $30/mo, $79/mo or $299/mo, with 30% off the first month for new customers on monthly plans. The All-in-One plans with lead credits and LinkedIn are $169/mo (Growth), $319/mo (Scale) and $639/mo (Ultimate) at their base email volumes. The CRM alone is $40/mo and the dialer alone is $29/mo.

Does SalesTarget.ai include LinkedIn automation?

Yes. Growth includes 1 LinkedIn account, Scale 2 and Ultimate 3; extra accounts cost $49 each.

Can I get a refund?

SalesTarget.ai's pricing page says current billing cycles aren't refunded. You can cancel from the dashboard and no contract is required.
