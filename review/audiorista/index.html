Our Verdict

Audiorista lets you build your own branded mobile app for audio, video and PDF content without writing code. You can stream MP3s and HLS video, offer downloads, pull in RSS feeds, and sell in-app subscriptions through RevenueCat, with Zapier sync for web purchases. It suits podcasters, audiobook and course creators, and publishers who want their own app in the App Store and Google Play instead of relying on someone else's platform. Pricing starts with a $29/mo Demo plan for building and previewing (no publishing or payments), then Lite at $240/mo for published iOS and Android apps with in-app payments (up to 200 monthly active users and 100 titles), Business from $400/mo with a web app and per-user fees, and Enterprise from $5,000/mo. Lite has a 30-day free trial, but a credit card is required. The trade-offs: it's expensive for small creators, Lite caps users and titles, and there's no free plan. This verdict is built from Audiorista's own published pages — RankerToolAI has not run a hands-on lab test of Audiorista.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Audiorista free?

No. There is a 30-day free trial of the Lite plan (credit card required, no charge if you cancel before it ends), but no free plan.

How much does Audiorista cost?

Demo is $29/mo, Lite is $240/mo, Business starts at $400/mo per app plus a per-user fee, and Enterprise starts at $5,000/mo.

Can I sell subscriptions in my Audiorista app?

Yes. Lite and above support in-app subscriptions through RevenueCat, and Business adds web payments.

Who is Audiorista for?

Podcasters, audiobook and course creators, and publishers who want their own branded audio or video app.
