Our Verdict

ClipTalk is an AI video editor that does the editing for you: instead of dragging clips on a timeline yourself, you give it an idea, a script, an article or a link, and it plans, scripts, creates and edits a publish-ready short video for TikTok, Instagram Reels or YouTube Shorts, with AI voices, animated captions, B-roll and AI characters, including UGC-style presenters and characters built from a single photo. Its most distinctive part is the MCP server: you can connect ClipTalk to Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Claude Code and other MCP clients and make, edit and render videos from inside your AI assistant, using 24 tools that cover templates, characters, voices, media generation, rendering and cost estimates. On the Pro plan an agent can also find trending topics and create videos on a schedule. Pricing is simple: 500 free credits (about 5 videos, no card), then Starter and Pro both at $39/mo (Pro shown as a locked-in price instead of $59) and Ultra at $199/mo. The trade-offs: API and MCP access need Pro, credit costs per video vary by length and models, no refund policy is published, and it's a young product, so expect fast changes. This verdict is built from ClipTalk's own published pages — RankerToolAI has not run a hands-on lab test of ClipTalk.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is ClipTalk free?

Yes, to start. New accounts get 500 credits, which ClipTalk says is about 5 videos, with no credit card required.

How much does ClipTalk cost?

Starter is $39/mo for 8,000 credits, Pro is $39/mo for 14,000 credits (a locked-in price shown against $59), and Ultra is $199/mo for 50,000 credits.

Is ClipTalk a video editor?

Yes. ClipTalk is an AI video editor that does the work for you: it plans, scripts, creates and edits the video from your idea, script or link. You can then change it in plain language or in its layer-based editor.

Does ClipTalk work with Claude and ChatGPT?

Yes. ClipTalk's MCP server (https://www.cliptalk.pro/mcp) connects to Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Claude Code and other MCP clients. Connecting is free; making videos uses your ClipTalk credits. Full MCP access is listed on Pro.

Can I monetize ClipTalk videos?

ClipTalk's FAQ says yes, and all plans list 100% content ownership.
