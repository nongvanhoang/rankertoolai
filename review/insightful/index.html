Our Verdict

Insightful is a workforce analytics platform that tracks time, apps and websites, and work patterns so managers can see capacity, utilization and where hours go across office, remote and hybrid teams. The Analytics plan costs $8 per seat per month billed yearly, or $10 billed monthly, and covers time and attendance, app and website usage, capacity and workload reports, and screenshots. Insights ($24 per seat, yearly only, 50-seat minimum) adds People Intelligence reports on work patterns and workload balance, and Intelligence is custom-priced with process maps and automation recommendations. Analytics has a free trial with no credit card. The trade-offs: screen recording, on-demand screenshots and workspace security cost $4 per seat each, the jump from Analytics to Insights is large, the newer plans are yearly only, and any monitoring tool needs a clear, honest policy with your employees. This verdict is built from Insightful's own published pages — RankerToolAI has not run a hands-on lab test of Insightful.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Insightful free?

No free plan is listed, but you can try the Analytics plan free with no credit card. Insights and Intelligence start with a Work Audit instead.

How much does Insightful cost?

Analytics is $8 per seat per month billed yearly or $10 billed monthly. Insights is $24 per seat per month, billed yearly, with a 50-seat minimum. Intelligence is priced on request.

Can Insightful take screenshots?

Yes. Screenshots are included in Workforce Analytics; screen recording, on-demand screenshots and higher-frequency screenshots are paid add-ons.

Is employee monitoring legal?

It depends on your country and how you use it. Tell your team what is tracked and why, and check local rules before you roll out any monitoring tool.
