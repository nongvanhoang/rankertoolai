Our Verdict

PostSnag solves a narrow but real problem: Facebook doesn't give marketers a good native way to save and organize posts they want to learn from later. As a Chrome extension, it captures any post type (text, photo, video, reel, link, album) along with engagement metrics, and lets you sort captures into boards or browse a community library of what's already working for other creators. The JSON export for AI tools is a nice touch for anyone feeding swiped examples into a writing assistant. The main open question is the free plan's exact limits, which PostSnag doesn't spell out clearly on its pricing page — worth checking directly before assuming it covers your use case. The $139 lifetime option is attractive if you're confident you'll use this long-term. This verdict is built from PostSnag's own published pricing and feature pages — RankerToolAI has not run a hands-on lab test of PostSnag.

Best PostSnag Alternatives

PostSnag isn't the only option in this space. Here's how the leading alternatives compare:

Frequently Asked Questions

Is PostSnag worth it?

For a marketer, agency, or creator who studies competitor and top-performing Facebook content regularly, PostSnag replaces ad-hoc screenshotting with a structured, searchable swipe file that keeps engagement metrics attached. It's a narrow tool, so it's worth it specifically if Facebook content research is a recurring task, not a one-off.

Does PostSnag have a free plan?

Yes, a free plan exists, though PostSnag's pricing page doesn't clearly spell out its exact limits — check directly on their site before assuming it covers your volume of captures.

Is PostSnag a lifetime deal?

Yes — alongside the $19/mo Pro subscription, PostSnag offers a $139 one-time Lifetime plan, a pricing model most competitors in this space have moved away from.

Does PostSnag work on platforms other than Facebook?

Based on its published feature set, PostSnag is currently Facebook-specific (posts, groups, reels) — there's no indication of Instagram, TikTok, or other platform support yet.

What are the best PostSnag alternatives?

Foreplay is a more established swipe-file tool aimed at ad creatives, generally pricier and broader than Facebook-only. Manual screenshotting/bookmarking remains the free fallback PostSnag is designed to replace.
