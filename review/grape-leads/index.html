Our Verdict

Grape Leads is a small Canadian lead-finding tool for web designers and SEO agencies. It searches Google Maps for local businesses that have no website, an outdated site or weak local SEO, and lets you filter by whether they're operating, have a phone number, or have any or recent reviews. It can batch-analyze business websites and produce SEO reports you can use in a pitch, enrich leads with details such as whether a phone line is mobile or landline, and export lists to CSV or send them to a webhook such as Zapier. There's no monthly fee: credit packs cost $28 for 50 credits, $55 for 100 and $127 for 250, and each search uses 1 credit plus 1 for every matching lead. The trade-offs: credits go quickly on broad searches, parts of the website haven't been updated in a long time and still describe a 2.0 app as upcoming, and the leads are only a starting point — cold outreach has to follow anti-spam rules where your prospects are. This verdict is built from Grape Leads' own published pages — RankerToolAI has not run a hands-on test of Grape Leads.
