Our Verdict

MentionAgent bundles the whole link building outreach stack into one subscription: it registers a separate sending domain, sets up and warms up a Gmail inbox on it, finds blog posts in your niche that could mention you, looks up and verifies the editor's email, writes a pitch about that specific post, follows up, and drafts your replies right through to a live link. Twice a month it also checks whether ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini mention you. Against buying an outreach tool, warmup service, email finder and inboxes separately, $99/mo is genuinely cheap, and keeping cold email off your main domain is the right call. Two things to weigh: it's built mainly for B2B SaaS founders and small agencies, and its model leans on mention swaps (you add their mention in return) — reciprocal linking at scale is something Google's spam policies warn about, so use the swap feature sparingly and stick to genuinely relevant placements. This verdict is built from MentionAgent's own published pricing and feature pages — RankerToolAI has not run a hands-on lab test of MentionAgent.

What MentionAgent Offers

AI Prospecting & Qualification

Finds posts that already rank for your buyers' searches, checks each site is worth pitching, and filters out paid link lists and link farms.

Contact Lookup & Verification

Finds the right editor's email and verifies it before anything is sent, which keeps bounce rates — and inbox reputation — healthy.

Personalized Pitch Drafting

Writes each pitch about the specific post it found. Approve every email yourself or switch on autopilot to send daily; about 2,500 drafts/month on the plan's 5,000 credits.

Managed Sending Infrastructure

Registers and pays for a separate sending domain, sets up a Gmail inbox with SPF/DKIM/DMARC, warms it up, and pauses if bounce rates climb — up to 1,200 outreach emails/month once warm.

Reply Handling to Live Link

When an editor replies, it drafts your answer with the exact page, paragraph and sentence for your link, follows up if they go quiet, and records the link once it's live.

AI Visibility Tracking & MCP

Twice a month asks ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini your buyers' questions and shows every answer that mentions you; also works from Claude, Cursor or any MCP agent.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is MentionAgent worth it?

For a SaaS founder or small agency with no time to run outreach, MentionAgent replaces roughly $700+/mo of separate tools and freelance pitch-writing for $99/mo. It's worth it if you approve drafts carefully and use mention swaps sparingly; it's not a fit if you need many sites or inboxes on one account.

Is MentionAgent a link building agency?

No — it's software you run yourself. Nobody buys links or writes guest posts on your behalf; the AI agent finds blogs, writes pitches and drafts replies, and you approve what gets sent.

Will MentionAgent hurt my email domain?

It's designed not to: outreach goes out from a separate domain and inbox that MentionAgent registers and warms up for you, so your main website and email never send cold email.

Does MentionAgent have a free trial?

You can see real outreach drafts for your site free, with no credit card required. Paid plans come with a 14-day money-back guarantee.

How does MentionAgent help with AI search (AEO)?

AI assistants like ChatGPT and Gemini lean on pages they already cite. MentionAgent targets those pages for mentions, then checks twice a month whether the assistants now mention you or cite a post you were featured in. See our guide to getting recommended by ChatGPT.
