Our Verdict

CustomGPT.ai lets a business build AI agents that answer only from its own content: websites and sitemaps, PDFs, Office files and 1,400+ formats, YouTube videos and podcasts, help centres and tools such as Google Drive, Notion, Confluence, Zendesk and HubSpot. Every answer links to its source, and the company leans hard on anti-hallucination and security (SOC 2 Type 2, GDPR, no training on your data). Agents can be embedded on a website, deployed to Slack or reached through a RAG API. Standard is $99 a month ($89 billed annually) for 10 agents, 1,000 queries and 60 million words of storage with 3 team members; Premium is $499 a month ($449 billed annually) for 25 agents, 5,000 queries, auto-sync of website content and white-labelling. The trade-offs: there is no free plan (the 7-day trial needs a credit card and converts automatically), queries are capped and agents stop answering when you hit the limit, add-ons are expensive (2,500 extra queries cost $375/mo), removing the “Powered by” badge needs Premium, and PayPal is not accepted. For a support, sales or knowledge-base assistant that must cite sources, it is a strong pick; for casual personal use it is overkill. This verdict is built from CustomGPT.ai's own published pages — RankerToolAI has not run a hands-on lab test of CustomGPT.ai.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is CustomGPT.ai free?

No free plan, but Standard and Premium both start with a 7-day free trial. A credit card is required and the trial converts automatically.

How much does CustomGPT.ai cost?

Standard is $99/mo ($89 billed annually) and Premium is $499/mo ($449 billed annually). Enterprise is custom.

What happens when I run out of queries?

Agents stop responding to new queries until the next cycle or until you add more queries or upgrade.

Can I remove the CustomGPT.ai branding?

Yes, white-labelling is included from the Premium plan up.

Does CustomGPT.ai train on my data?

CustomGPT.ai says it does not train on or share your data, and it is SOC 2 Type 2 and GDPR compliant.
