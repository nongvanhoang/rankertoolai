Our Verdict

Syncee is a dropshipping and wholesale marketplace connecting two genuinely different audiences: retailers who want to import products to sell, and suppliers who want their catalog placed in front of retailers. For retailers, the pitch is a large, vetted catalog (8M+ products from 12,000+ suppliers across the US, EU, UK, CA, and AU) with 0% transaction fees on every plan — a real advantage over competitors that take a cut per order. The tradeoff is that the entry-level paid plan only covers 25 imported products, so most stores will land on a $60-100/mo tier once they need a real catalog. For suppliers, it's effectively risk-free to try — free to join, no listing fees, and you set your own margins. This verdict is built from Syncee's published pricing, feature set, and independent third-party reviews — RankerToolAI has not run a hands-on lab test of Syncee.

What Syncee Offers

A Large, Vetted Product Catalog

Syncee connects retailers to over 8 million products from more than 12,000 vetted suppliers based primarily in the US, EU, UK, Canada, and Australia. Retailers can browse by category, supplier location, or shipping region, and build catalogs of products to import into their store.

Automated Sync for Inventory, Pricing & Orders

Once products are imported, Syncee keeps inventory levels and pricing updated automatically — Pro-tier and above get up to twice-daily auto-updates. Order routing forwards retailer sales to the relevant supplier for fulfillment without manual handling on either side.

Native Ecommerce Platform Integrations

Syncee connects directly to Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, Wix, and other major platforms, so imported products, stock levels, and pricing rules sync into the store's existing product catalog rather than requiring a separate system.

Dynamic Pricing & Catalog Tools

Retailers can set markup rules (percentage or fixed) that apply automatically as supplier prices change, and organize sourced products into multiple catalogs — useful for testing different niches or supplier relationships without manually re-pricing every item.

How the Supplier Side Works

Suppliers — brands, manufacturers, or wholesalers with their own product catalog — can join Syncee for free, with no listing fees. After onboarding, a supplier's catalog becomes discoverable to Syncee's retailer network, who can import and sell those products in their own stores. Suppliers set their own wholesale pricing; Syncee's own guidance suggests most suppliers price to leave retailers roughly a 30-50% margin, which affects how attractive a listing looks to retailers browsing the marketplace. Order and inventory sync works the same way in both directions — when a retailer sells a supplier's product, the order routes back to the supplier for fulfillment.

Best Syncee Alternatives

Syncee isn't the only dropshipping and wholesale marketplace. Here's how the leading alternatives compare:

  • Spocket — Heavily focused on US/EU suppliers with fast shipping and branded invoicing, priced similarly to Syncee's mid tiers, but with a smaller overall catalog.
  • SaleHoo — More of a supplier directory than a live sync platform — useful for researching suppliers manually, but without Syncee's automated inventory/pricing sync.
  • Modalyst — Tightly integrated with Wix, mixing dropship and print-on-demand suppliers, with a catalog considerably smaller than Syncee's 8M+ products.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Syncee worth it?

For retailers sourcing products, yes — 8M+ products from 12,000+ vetted suppliers with 0% transaction fees on every plan is a strong catalog, and the free Starter tier lets you browse before paying. The tradeoff is that retailer plan pricing climbs quickly: Basic ($40/mo) only covers 25 imported products, so a store needing a real catalog will likely land on Pro ($60/mo) or higher. For suppliers, it's effectively free to try — no listing fees, and you set your own margins.

What is the difference between the two Syncee referral links?

Syncee runs separate signup paths for its two audiences. Retailers are online stores that want to import and sell products sourced from Syncee's supplier network. Suppliers are brands or wholesalers that want to list their own catalog so retailers can source and sell it. The two paths lead to different account types, different onboarding flows, and different pricing structures on Syncee — they are not two links for the same action.

How much does Syncee cost for retailers?

Syncee's Starter plan is free but does not allow product imports — it's for browsing the catalog. Basic is about $40/month for up to 25 imported products. Pro is about $60/month for up to 250 products, 20 catalogs, and twice-daily inventory auto-updates. Business is about $100/month for up to 10,000 products and 50 catalogs. Plus is about $299/month for up to 50,000 products and unlimited catalogs. All paid plans have 0% transaction fees and free trials are available on most tiers.

Is it free to join Syncee as a supplier?

Yes — suppliers can join Syncee's marketplace for free, with no listing fees. Suppliers set their own wholesale pricing, and Syncee's guidance suggests most suppliers price to leave retailers a 30-50% margin. Suppliers earn from the products retailers sell, not from a Syncee subscription fee.

What are the best Syncee alternatives?

Spocket focuses heavily on US/EU suppliers with fast shipping and branded invoicing, at a similar price point to Syncee's mid tiers. SaleHoo is a supplier directory rather than a live product-sync platform, better suited to research than automated inventory management. Modalyst integrates tightly with Wix and offers a mix of dropship and print-on-demand suppliers, with a smaller catalog than Syncee's 8M+ products.
