Should you pay for Synthesia?

Try the free plan first, then pay only if your team makes training or onboarding videos from scripts every month. Pro at $89 a month ($59 yearly) fits that work better than Starter, whose credits add only about 12 minutes of video a month, or about 2 Syren videos. Skip it if you want to make many videos with the Syren assistant on a small budget, or if you need SCORM export without an Enterprise contract.

What we actually tested: one 5-sentence onboarding script on a free Basic account on 5 November 2026, screen-recorded from start to finish, with no card entered. We did not test paid plans, SCORM export, or making a Personal Avatar. Feature and price details for those come from Synthesia's own pages and are marked as such.

How Syren Turned 5 Sentences Into a Video

Syren is Synthesia's AI video agent, marked New in the app. We pasted our script into the box on the home page and sent it. Syren then offered three story directions and asked us to pick one.

After that it worked alone: it created a presenter image, generated each spoken take, then built the scene. The chat panel listed three build steps of 17 seconds, 3 minutes 10 seconds and 57 seconds. From sending the script to a playable preview took about 5.5 minutes. That is one run on one short script, so yours may differ.

Syren kept our sentences as written. It only added delivery cues such as [warmly] and [short pause] to the script.

The Catch: Syren Uses Far More Credits

This is the number to check before you pay. When our video finished, the chat panel showed 558.2 credits used, more than the 500 we started with.

Synthesia's pricing page explains most of it. Basic includes one Syren video as a limited promo, with 1,000 Syren-only promo credits listed. So those 558 credits probably did not come from our 500, though the page does not say which pool is used first.

What the Free Plan Gives You

Our account started with 500 credits. Synthesia lists these monthly on Basic, along with 10 minutes of Studio video or dubbing and 3 survey responses.

Avatars: we counted 12 stock avatars in the Express-3 list, including a robot and two stylised characters. Synthesia's pricing page says Basic includes 9, so the number you see may differ.

Downloads: the old version of this review said the free plan could not download. That was wrong for our account. We downloaded the Syren video in Full HD, and the file name itself says "watermarked". Synthesia's pricing page still lists "Download your content" under Starter, and we only tried one Syren video, so check this on your own account.

Your own avatar: the Avatars page showed an "Upgrade to create Avatar" button. Synthesia lists 3 Personal Avatars on Starter and 5 on Pro. We stopped at the intro screen, so we cannot tell you how good a Personal Avatar looks.

Avatar Builder: this tool was open to us. You pick style, age, vibe, lens and framing, and the button showed 28 credits for 2 looks. We got still images only and have not made one talk, so treat it as a way to design a look, not a finished presenter.

Watch Out: The Template Assistant Rewrites Your Script

We also pasted the same 5 sentences into a 13-scene template through the Assistant, marked Beta. It built a 4-part outline, then wrote a longer script scene by scene. "If anything is unclear, message your team lead" became "If anything feels unclear, message your team lead first. That's the quickest way to get unstuck and keep moving with confidence."

A rewrite can help when you only have rough notes. If your wording has been approved by legal or HR, read every scene before you generate, or use the route that keeps your text.

Languages

Synthesia advertises 140+ languages. In October we pasted a Spanish translation into an English scene on the free plan and generated it again. The presenter spoke Spanish, but text on the slides stayed in English, so you need to translate those yourself. Have a native speaker check the result before you publish.

Who Should Buy It

  • Good fit: HR and L&D teams making onboarding or training videos from approved scripts, where nobody wants to be on camera and videos need regular updates.
  • Good fit on Enterprise: larger teams that need SCORM, SSO and live collaboration. Synthesia says it is trusted by 50,000+ teams and over 90% of Fortune 100 companies.
  • Poor fit: anyone planning to make many videos with Syren on Starter, since the credits cover only about 2 videos.
  • Poor fit: small teams that need SCORM but cannot justify an Enterprise contract.
  • Poor fit: anyone who needs no Synthesia logo on a $0 budget. The logo comes off from Starter.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Synthesia free?

Yes. The Basic plan costs $0 and lists 10 minutes of Studio video or dubbing a month, 500 credits a month and one Syren video as a limited promo. Videos carry a Synthesia watermark.

Can you download videos on Synthesia's free plan?

On our Basic account we could. On 5 November 2026 we downloaded one Syren video as a 1920x1080 MP4 with the Synthesia watermark in it. Synthesia's pricing page lists downloads under Starter, so this may not apply to every video type or last.

How many credits does a Synthesia Syren video use?

Synthesia says a one-minute Syren video typically uses 1,000 to 2,000 credits, while a typical finished minute of standard video uses about 100. Our video, about 36 seconds long, showed 558.2 credits used.

How long does Synthesia take to make a video?

In our one test with Syren, it took about 5.5 minutes from sending the script to a playable preview of a video about 36 seconds long. We did not time standard Studio generation in this test.

Do Synthesia credits roll over?

No. Synthesia says monthly allowances and credits reset each billing period. On annual plans the year's credits are available upfront. Credits bought separately last 12 months.

Is Synthesia better than HeyGen?

We have not tested them side by side. On their pricing pages, Synthesia starts cheaper at $19 a month against $29 for HeyGen Creator. HeyGen lists SCORM export on its $149 Business plan, while Synthesia lists it on custom-priced Enterprise.
