Our Verdict

ImagineArt is an AI creative platform that brings image generation, video generation, editing and audio creation together under one credit system. Every tool spends the same credits, so one subscription covers images and video. Basic costs $5/mo for 1,500 credits, but its image generations are public. Ultimate ($50/mo, or $34/mo billed yearly) gives 16,000 credits, private generations and more concurrent jobs. Creator ($120/mo, $83 yearly) adds 45,000 credits and unlimited realtime generations, and Scale ($500/mo) is for heavy production. One-time credit packs from $10 top you up when you run out. The trade-offs: advanced models burn credits much faster than the basic estimates, Basic images are public, prices vary by country, and the pricing page doesn't state a refund policy. This verdict is built from ImagineArt's own published pricing — RankerToolAI has not run a hands-on lab test of ImagineArt.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does ImagineArt cost?

Basic is $5/mo, Ultimate is $50/mo ($34/mo billed yearly), Creator is $120/mo ($83 yearly) and Scale is $500/mo ($350 yearly), at US list prices.

Are ImagineArt images private?

Not on Basic, where image generations are public. Ultimate and Creator list private generations.

What happens when I run out of ImagineArt credits?

Your plan stays active. You can buy a one-time credit pack or wait for next month's allowance.

Can I use ImagineArt images commercially?

Paid plans list ImagineArt's General Commercial Terms. Read those terms for the details before you use images in client work.
