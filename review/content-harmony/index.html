Our Verdict

Content Harmony is built around one job: turning a keyword into a brief a writer can use. Each Content Workflow includes a keyword report (search intent, overlapping keywords and entities, competitor structure, questions and sources), a shareable content brief, and a content grader that scores a draft against a topic model. Pricing depends only on how many workflows you need each month. The smallest plan is $50 a month for 5 workflows (about $42 billed annually), then $99 for 12, $199 for 25, $299 for 50, $599 for 100 and $799 for 150, with Enterprise from $1,000 a month. Every plan includes unlimited users, unlimited projects and free team training, and monthly credits stay valid for 90 days. The trial is unusual: you pay $10 for 10 workflows, there is no time limit and it does not renew automatically. The trade-offs: cost per workflow is high if you publish a lot of short pages (about $8 to $10 each on the small plans), there is no free plan, and it is a research and briefing tool rather than a full rank tracker or backlink suite. It suits agencies and in-house teams that brief freelance writers. Solo bloggers who mostly want a content score may find a cheaper optimizer enough. This verdict is built from Content Harmony's published pages — RankerToolAI has not run a hands-on lab test of Content Harmony.
