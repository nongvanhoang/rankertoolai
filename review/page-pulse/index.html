Our Verdict

Page Pulse is a lightweight website analytics tool for marketers, founders and agencies who find Google Analytics 4 too complicated. You add your site, paste one small script (under 3 KB by the vendor's count) and get visitors, traffic channels, automatic click tracking and conversions in one dashboard, without setting up events or custom reports. AI Insights grades pages and points out issues, and higher plans add heatmaps, page notes and comments, event-based and cross-domain conversions and a live map. Every plan includes unlimited collaborators, so clients and teammates can view data for free. Pricing (monthly): Free $0 (1 page, 14 days of data, 15 AI insights), Starter $29 (2 pages, 90 days, conversions), Growth $79 (5 pages, 2 heatmaps, 1 year), Pro $129 (10 pages, 5 heatmaps, 3 years) and Scale $199 (20 pages, 15 heatmaps); annual billing saves 20%. The trade-offs: plans are capped by tracked pages, so check what counts as a page before you buy; the free plan keeps only 14 days of data; heatmaps start at $79/mo; and it is a young product with far fewer reports than GA4. This verdict is built from Page Pulse's own published pages — RankerToolAI has not run a hands-on test of Page Pulse.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Page Pulse free?

Yes. The free-forever plan tracks 1 page with basic analytics, 14 days of data and 15 AI insights a month, with no credit card.

How much does Page Pulse cost?

Starter is $29/mo, Growth $79/mo, Pro $129/mo and Scale $199/mo. Annual billing saves 20%.

Can Page Pulse replace Google Analytics?

For simple traffic, click and conversion tracking it can. If you rely on GA4's advanced reports, funnels or BigQuery exports, Page Pulse is much lighter.

Does Page Pulse have heatmaps?

Yes, from the Growth plan: 2 heatmaps on Growth, 5 on Pro and 15 on Scale.
