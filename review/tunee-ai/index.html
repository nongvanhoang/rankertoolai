Our Verdict

Tunee is a conversational AI music and music-video generator: describe a mood or upload a reference, and it produces a song by routing through a mix of underlying models (Mureka, Minimax, TemPolor, Ace-Step, Kling), then can turn that track into a video with lip-sync and AI-generated visuals. The feature set is genuinely differentiated — voice cloning, stem separation, and bundled video generation aren't all standard even among AI music tools. The catch is trust data: Tunee's Trustpilot page has only 4 reviews at the time of writing, far too thin to draw a real conclusion from, and independent reviews we found document specific reliability issues — quality degradation during high-traffic periods, daily credits that expire unused rather than rolling over, and a rights system where free-tier tracks don't automatically carry commercial ownership even after upgrading. This verdict is built from Tunee's published feature set and pricing plus third-party reviews — RankerToolAI has not run a hands-on lab test of Tunee.

⚠ What to know before you pay

Tunee's public review trail is thin, so treat any star rating (ours included) with caution. What we found in independent write-ups: unused daily credits do not roll over ("use-it-or-lose-it"), free-tier generations don't automatically carry commercial rights even after you upgrade to a paid plan (you claim older tracks individually, limited to 1-3 claims/month depending on tier), and output quality is reported to dip during high-traffic periods. A couple of Trustpilot reviewers also describe the app deducting credits at the video-generation step and then failing, with slow support response. None of this means the product doesn't work — but confirm the credit and ownership terms on Tunee's own pricing page before committing to a paid tier, and start on the free plan first.

What Tunee AI Offers

Conversational Music Creation

Instead of technical prompts or a DAW interface, Tunee works as a chat-based creative agent — describe a mood, upload a reference track, or share a clip, and it generates a song. Under the hood it routes through a mix of AI models (Mureka, Minimax, TemPolor, Ace-Step, Kling) rather than a single proprietary engine.

Voice Cloning & Stem Separation

Voice cloning lets you maintain a consistent vocal identity across multiple tracks. Stem separation splits a finished track into 2, 4, or 6 components for remixing or further production work outside Tunee.

Music Videos, Lip-Sync & AI Characters

Tunee converts a track into a music video with AI-generated visuals, and can animate a photo with realistic lip-sync. Motion control directs camera angles and character movement, and AI character creation lets you build a persistent musical persona to reuse across releases.

Mastering & Distribution-Ready Output

Paid plans include studio-quality mastering optimized for streaming platforms, plus commercial licensing intended to support direct upload to Spotify, YouTube, and TikTok.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Tunee AI worth it?

For quickly turning an idea into a finished song and music video without production skills, Tunee is genuinely capable — the conversational workflow, voice cloning, and stem separation are real, differentiated features. The catch is a very thin public review sample (4 Trustpilot reviews) and multiple independent write-ups documenting reliability issues: quality drops during high-traffic periods, unused daily credits expiring rather than rolling over, and free-tier tracks not automatically carrying commercial rights even after you upgrade. Worth trialing on the free plan before committing to a paid tier.

What does Tunee AI actually do?

Tunee is a conversational AI music and music-video generator. You describe a mood, upload a reference track, or share a clip, and it generates a song using a blend of underlying AI models (Mureka, Minimax, TemPolor, Ace-Step, Kling). It can also convert that track into a music video with AI-generated visuals, lip-sync animation of a photo, and voice cloning to keep a consistent vocal identity across tracks.

Does Tunee AI have a free plan?

Yes — Tunee's free plan includes a daily credit allowance for generating tracks, but output is for non-commercial use only. Paid plans start around $18/month (Basic) and add commercial licensing, more monthly credits, and access to more AI agents/conversations.

Why is Tunee AI's Trustpilot rating so low?

Tunee sits at roughly 2.7/5 on Trustpilot, but from only 4 reviews at the time of writing — too small a sample to treat as a reliable signal in either direction. The reviews that do exist describe the app deducting credits at the video-generation step and then failing, and difficulty reaching support. Independent reviews separately describe quality degradation during peak usage and a credit/ownership system that requires attention (unused daily credits expire, and older free-tier tracks need to be individually claimed after upgrading).

What are the best Tunee AI alternatives?

Suno and Udio are the best-known AI music generators, both with larger, more established user bases and public track records than Tunee. Neither bundles music-video generation (lip-sync, AI character personas) the way Tunee does, so the right choice depends on whether you need a finished video or just the audio.
