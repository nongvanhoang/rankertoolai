What is Reditus?

Reditus is a partner-program platform for one kind of business: B2B SaaS. It lets a software company run two growth channels from one dashboard. The first is an affiliate program, where outside publishers, creators and consultants earn a recurring commission for customers they send. The second is an in-app referral program, where your existing customers invite others from inside your product.

The B2B SaaS focus shapes the product. Commissions are tied to subscription revenue, so Reditus connects to billing systems (Stripe, Paddle, Chargebee) to track renewals, calculate commissions and pay them out. It also shapes the network: because every program on Reditus is B2B SaaS, affiliates who join tend to be people whose audience buys software, which is the audience a SaaS company wants.

Reditus says its network has generated $11M+ for partners and that 200+ B2B SaaS teams use it. One published customer story is Joiin, a financial consolidation tool we've reviewed, which credits Reditus with building an affiliate channel from zero to more than €130K ARR. These are vendor claims; we haven't verified them independently.

Key Features

1. Affiliate program toolkit

Every plan includes unlimited affiliates, campaigns, multiple affiliate tiers (so top partners can earn a higher rate), Stripe coupon tracking for affiliates who promote with discount codes, and fraud detection. Reditus says installation support is included, which matters because tracking setup is where most affiliate programs go wrong.

2. In-app referral program

The referral side gives you unlimited advocates, a customisable embed inside your app, double-sided benefits (the referrer and the new customer both get something) and UID tracking, which Reditus says works without passing personal data. Running affiliates and customer referrals in one tool means one set of payouts and one report rather than two systems.

3. Automated payouts

Reditus calculates commissions from your billing data and pays affiliates for you. That saves a monthly spreadsheet-and-PayPal routine, but it isn't free: see payout fees below.

4. AI affiliate recruitment

Reditus includes an AI search over its affiliate database for finding partners who fit your product: 1 search a month with limited database access on Growth, 5 a month with full access on Scale Up, and unlimited on Enterprise. Scale Up and Enterprise also include a vetted affiliate database.

5. Integrations

Reditus lists integrations with Stripe, Paddle and Chargebee for billing, HubSpot for CRM, plus Slack and User.com. Make and Zapier are listed as coming soon. Reditus says other payment processors can connect via its API.

6. Free migration

If you already run a program elsewhere, Reditus offers free white-glove migration with any annual plan, including Growth billed annually. It says its team handles the data import and tracking setup, that existing affiliates keep their links, and that there is a 30-day cookie transition window.

The Affiliate Marketplace

The marketplace is the main reason to choose Reditus over a tracking-only tool. SaaS companies list their programs; affiliates browse them, apply from one dashboard and, once approved, get a tracking link. Reditus says the network reaches 27,000+ B2B SaaS affiliates. For a SaaS company with no partners yet, the hardest part of an affiliate program is usually recruitment, not tracking, and the marketplace is meant to solve that.

The plan details matter here. The marketplace listing, public campaigns, the vetted affiliate database and full AI search access all start on Scale Up, which is $399/mo and annual-only, and Reditus says listings go live once the annual contract starts. On Growth you get the full tracking and payout toolkit and 1 AI affiliate search a month, but you recruit affiliates yourself. In other words, the feature that most sets Reditus apart costs $4,788 a year.

Payout Fees & Revenue Caps

Two costs sit outside the plan price. First, automated payouts carry a fee on every plan: 5% when you fund payouts by credit card, 2% by invoice. On $2,000 of monthly affiliate commissions, that is $100 a month by card or $40 by invoice, so paying by invoice is worth setting up once your program grows.

Second, Growth and Scale Up each have a combined revenue cap: $60K ARR and $360K ARR. Reditus's pricing page doesn't spell out exactly which revenue counts toward the cap, so if you're near either threshold, ask Reditus before you choose a plan. The jump from Growth to Scale Up is large, from $99 to $399 a month on annual billing.

Reditus From the Affiliate Side

This is the part of Reditus we have used directly. RankerToolAI joined Reditus as an affiliate and promotes several programs listed on it, including Joiin, Parallel AI and siift. Joining is free for affiliates, since Reditus is paid by the SaaS companies. From one dashboard you can browse programs, apply, see pending and active partnerships, and copy a tracking link for each approved program.

What stood out to us is how clearly each program shows its terms: commission rate, commission length, cookie duration, payout threshold and, importantly, a paid-ads policy. That last field matters for publishers who run search ads, because some programs on Reditus allow every kind of paid ad while others ban bidding on the brand name. For a SaaS company, this means your terms are visible to affiliates before they apply, which should cut down on rule-breaking partners.

We can't yet comment on payout reliability from experience, because we haven't reached a payout threshold. We'll update this section when we do.

Who Should Use Reditus?

Based on its published features and pricing, Reditus fits these companies best:

  • B2B SaaS companies starting an affiliate program from zero that can budget for Scale Up. The marketplace gives you a pool of affiliates to recruit from instead of cold outreach.
  • SaaS teams that want customer referrals and affiliates in one tool. One dashboard, one payout process.
  • Companies on Stripe, Paddle or Chargebee that want commissions calculated from real subscription revenue, including renewals.
  • Teams switching from another affiliate platform that would rather have Reditus move their data and links than do it themselves.

Who should skip it: B2C, ecommerce and one-off-purchase businesses, which Reditus isn't built for; SaaS companies that already have an established partner base and only need tracking, which can use Growth but may find cheaper tracking-only tools; and very early startups that can't yet afford Scale Up, since on Growth you recruit affiliates yourself.

Reditus Alternatives

We haven't published reviews of other affiliate management platforms yet, so we're naming the main options without a full comparison. When you compare, check three things: whether the platform brings you affiliates or only tracks them, how payout fees work, and whether pricing scales with your revenue.

  • PartnerStack is a partner platform with its own marketplace, also aimed at B2B SaaS, and generally pitched at larger programs.
  • Rewardful, FirstPromoter and Tolt are SaaS affiliate tracking tools built around Stripe and similar billing systems. They focus on tracking and payouts rather than supplying a marketplace of affiliates.
