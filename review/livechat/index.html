Our Verdict

LiveChat is a mature, established live-chat widget (in the market since 2002) with AI-driven chat routing, canned responses, and integrations for the major CRM/helpdesk/e-commerce platforms. The 14-day free trial requires no credit card, so there's no real barrier to testing it against your own traffic before paying. The tradeoff is trust: LiveChat sits at a below-average 2.8/5 on Trustpilot from 96 reviews, and the recurring complaint theme is billing — customers report being charged before an invoice arrives and being billed after cancellation with refunds denied. This verdict is built from LiveChat's published feature set, pricing, and independent third-party reviews — RankerToolAI has not run a hands-on lab test of LiveChat.

⚠ Before you enter card details

The most consistent complaint pattern in LiveChat's Trustpilot reviews is around billing: payments processed before an invoice is issued (unusual compared to typical SaaS billing flows), and some reviewers reporting they were charged after canceling with refund requests denied. This doesn't appear to reflect the chat widget's actual functionality — longer-tenured users describe the product itself favorably. Read the cancellation terms in your plan before committing, and confirm cancellation in writing.

What LiveChat Offers

LiveChat is a website chat widget aimed at sales and support teams. LiveChat's Sister Products: ChatBot & HelpDesk

LiveChat, ChatBot, and HelpDesk are all built by the same company (Text, Inc.) and sold separately, each with its own pricing and free trial. Here's an honest look at the other two, in case one fits your situation better than live chat alone.

ChatBot — AI Chatbot Builder

⭐ 2.2/5 Trustpilot (13 reviews — a small sample, treat this rating with caution either way)

ChatBot lets you build automated conversation flows and AI agents that handle common questions without a human agent, then hand off to a live agent (e.g., via LiveChat) for anything the bot can't resolve.
