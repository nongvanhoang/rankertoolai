Our Verdict

GetResponse has evolved from a standard email service provider into a broader marketing platform: AI email and subject-line generation, automation workflows, abandoned-cart recovery, and — on the Creator plan — a website builder, course creator, and webinar tool bundled in. For a team that wants automation and AI drafting without stitching together three or four separate tools, the Marketer plan is a strong consolidation play. The catch is the Starter tier: its AI generation is capped at 3 uses/month, which works fine to evaluate quality but not to actually run an AI-assisted content workflow, and the step up to Marketer is a meaningful price jump. This verdict is built from GetResponse's published pricing, documented feature set, and independent third-party reviews — RankerToolAI has not run a hands-on lab test of GetResponse.

What GetResponse Offers

AI Email & Subject-Line Generation

GetResponse's AI tools draft full email copy and subject lines from a short prompt, built directly into the campaign editor rather than a separate app. On Starter, this is capped at 3 generations per month — useful to test output quality, but not enough to run an AI-assisted content workflow. Marketer and Creator plans remove the cap entirely.

Marketing Automation Workflows

Starter includes exactly 1 automation workflow — enough for a basic welcome sequence. Marketer and above unlock unlimited workflows with a visual, behavior-based builder: trigger sequences off email opens, link clicks, purchases, or custom events without needing a separate automation platform bolted on.

Abandoned Cart Recovery & Sales Funnels

Available from the Marketer tier up, GetResponse's ecommerce features track cart abandonment and trigger recovery sequences automatically, plus a funnel builder for landing-page-to-checkout flows. This is a genuine differentiator versus email-only competitors that require a separate funnel tool.

Website, Course & Webinar Bundle (Creator Plan)

The Creator tier adds a website builder, a course-creation tool supporting up to 500 students, and webinar hosting for up to 100 attendees, plus premium newsletter subscription support. For a solo creator or small team, this can replace 2-3 separate subscriptions (site builder, course host, webinar tool) with one bill — the tradeoff is a steeper price than Marketer.

Enterprise Tier

Custom-priced, Enterprise adds SMS marketing, mobile push notifications, single sign-on (SSO), a dedicated account manager, and unlimited user seats — aimed at organizations that have outgrown the 5-user cap on lower tiers.

Best GetResponse Alternatives

GetResponse isn't the only option for AI-assisted email marketing. Here's how the leading alternatives compare:

  • Mailchimp — A broader third-party integration ecosystem, but typically costs more per contact as your list grows past a few thousand subscribers.
  • ActiveCampaign — Deeper CRM-style automation and lead scoring for sales-driven teams, with a steeper learning curve than GetResponse's builder.
  • beehiiv — A simpler, newsletter-first platform that's often cheaper for solo creators who just need a great newsletter without funnels, webinars, or deep automation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is GetResponse worth it?

For a team that wants email marketing, automation, and AI content drafting in one platform, yes. GetResponse's Marketer plan bundles unlimited automation workflows, full AI tools, and abandoned-cart/funnel features that would otherwise require stitching together several tools. The Starter plan's AI generation is capped at just 3 uses/month, so it functions more as a trial of the AI features than a usable tier for AI-driven workflows.

Does GetResponse have a free plan?

GetResponse's current published pricing does not list a permanent free plan — it starts at the paid Starter tier, with a free trial period available at signup. Confirm the current trial length directly on GetResponse's pricing page, as trial terms change.

What is the difference between GetResponse's plans?

Starter (~$16-19/mo billed annually) covers unlimited sends with only 3 AI generations/month and 1 automation workflow. Marketer (~$50-59/mo, most popular) unlocks unlimited automation workflows, full AI access, advanced segmentation, abandoned cart recovery, and sales funnels. Creator (~$58-69/mo) adds a website builder, course creator, and webinar hosting for up to 100 attendees. Enterprise is custom-priced and adds SMS/push notifications, SSO, and a dedicated account manager.

Does GetResponse's AI actually generate full campaigns?

Yes — GetResponse's AI tools draft email copy and subject lines from a prompt. On the Starter plan this is limited to 3 generations per month, which is enough to evaluate quality but not to run an AI-driven content workflow. Marketer and Creator plans unlock unlimited AI generation.

What are the best GetResponse alternatives?

Mailchimp offers a broader integration ecosystem but typically costs more per contact at scale. ActiveCampaign goes deeper on CRM-style automation and lead scoring, with a steeper learning curve. beehiiv is a simpler, newsletter-first alternative that's often cheaper for solo creators who don't need GetResponse's funnel and webinar features.

Is GetResponse good for small businesses or solo creators?

For a small business that wants automation and AI drafting without buying separate tools, the Marketer plan is a reasonable fit despite the price step-up from Starter. A solo creator focused purely on a newsletter, without needing funnels or automation, will likely find beehiiv or a similar newsletter-first tool cheaper for the same core job.
