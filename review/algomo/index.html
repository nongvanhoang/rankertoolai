Our Verdict

Algomo is an AI agent that lives on your website. It answers visitors using your site's own live data, can move around the page with the visitor, shows product cards, calendars and forms in chat, books meetings on Google and Outlook calendars, and hands the conversation to a human with email and Slack alerts. An unusual detail: you set it up by talking to Claude, ChatGPT or another coding assistant instead of clicking through a dashboard. Pricing is credit-based: a free-forever plan with 100 credits a month for one user, and a Business plan from $50/mo for 2,000 credits (or $500/yr, two months free) that scales up to 200,000 credits. The trade-offs: web chat is the only channel (no email, WhatsApp, SMS or voice), there are no top-ups, so the agent pauses when credits run out, and inactive free accounts are deleted after 14 days. This verdict is built from Algomo's own published pages — RankerToolAI has not run a hands-on lab test of Algomo.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Algomo free?

Yes. The Free plan is ongoing, not a trial: 100 credits a month for one user, one workspace and one agent, with no credit card.

How much does Algomo cost?

Business starts at $50/mo for 2,000 credits, or $500/yr. Larger credit packages go up to $4,300/mo for 200,000 credits. Enterprise is custom.

What happens when Algomo runs out of credits?

There are no top-ups or overage billing. The agent pauses until your credits renew.

Does Algomo work on WhatsApp or email?

No. Algomo works in web chat only.
