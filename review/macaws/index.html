Our Verdict

Macaws sells a bespoke, custom-trained AI voice receptionist that answers business calls 24/7, qualifies leads, and books appointments directly into a calendar or CRM — built specifically for small service businesses like dental practices, law firms, and clinics that lose bookings to missed calls. The mechanics are genuinely sound: 48-hour setup, a script you approve before going live, month-to-month billing with no setup fee and a 30-day cancellation notice. The honest catch is trust data — Macaws currently has just 1 rating on Trustpilot (3.7/5) and no G2 listing, which is nowhere near enough to draw a real conclusion from in either direction. This verdict is built from Macaws' published feature set, pricing, and the limited independent review data available — RankerToolAI has not run a hands-on test of Macaws.

⚠ Before you sign up

Macaws is a small, newer company with almost no independent review history — one Trustpilot rating isn't a statistically meaningful sample. The testimonials on Macaws' own site (a dental practice, a law firm, an aesthetics clinic) are real-sounding but unverified first-party claims, not independently audited case studies. Because plans run month-to-month with no long-term contract, the practical risk is low, but go in treating this as an early-stage vendor to trial cautiously, not a proven, widely-reviewed platform.

What Macaws Offers

A Custom-Trained AI Voice Receptionist

Macaws' pitch isn't a generic IVR bot — it's a bespoke AI persona trained specifically on one business's services, FAQs, booking flow, tone, and edge cases, built during a 30-minute onboarding call and reviewed/approved by the client before going live. That per-business training step is what separates it from a plug-and-play chatbot and is the main reason it can plausibly pass as human on a first call, per their own FAQ.

24/7 Answering, Booking & Lead Qualification

The AI answers inbound calls within seconds around the clock, captures and qualifies lead details, and books appointments directly by syncing with Google Calendar, Outlook, or Calendly. For queries it can't resolve, it offers to take a message, schedule a callback, or transfer to a human — it isn't positioned as a full human replacement for complex calls.

CRM Integration & Multi-Language Support

Higher tiers connect to HubSpot, Salesforce, and a claimed 4,000+ other apps via integrations/webhooks, plus automatic detection and switching across 30+ languages mid-call — useful for businesses with a multilingual customer base that a single-language bot script would miss.

Real-Time Dashboard & Data Handling

Calls, transcripts, and bookings are tracked in a live dashboard (app.macaws.ai). Macaws states all call data is processed and stored on UK/EU servers, encrypted in transit and at rest, and describes itself as fully GDPR compliant — relevant if you're a UK/EU business with data-residency requirements for customer call data.

Macaws Alternatives

Macaws isn't the only AI answering option. Depending on what you actually need, one of these may fit better:

  • LiveChat — Best if your business is primarily handling web/chat inquiries rather than phone calls; Macaws is voice-first, LiveChat is chat-first.
  • Generic AI phone-answering platforms (e.g. Smith.ai, Ruby) — Better if you want a larger, more established provider with a longer public review history, even if less bespoke per-business training.
  • A traditional answering service — Better if you need guaranteed human handling of every call rather than an AI-first approach, at typically higher per-minute cost.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Macaws AI receptionist worth it?

It's a reasonable option to trial if you're a small service business (dental, legal, medical, home services) losing bookings to missed calls, since the core mechanics — 48-hour custom setup, calendar sync, no lock-in contract — are genuinely useful and low-risk to cancel out of. The honest caveat is that Macaws has almost no independent review history yet (a single Trustpilot rating), so you're evaluating it mostly on its own claims and a short cancellation notice rather than a proven track record.

How much does Macaws AI receptionist cost?

Macaws runs three monthly plans: Virtual Front Desk at £99/mo (unlimited inbound calls, one AI persona, email summaries), Integrated Associate at £249/mo (adds CRM integration and review management, marked "most popular" on their site), and Growth Engine at £499/mo (adds outbound dialing and multi-channel retargeting). All are billed as monthly rolling contracts with no setup fee and a 30-day cancellation notice.

Does Macaws have real customer reviews?

As of August 2026, Macaws has only 1 rating on Trustpilot (3.7/5) and no listing found on G2. That's not enough data to draw a real conclusion from — it's a small, newer company, and a single review isn't statistically meaningful in either direction. The testimonials shown on Macaws' own site (a dental practice, a law firm, an aesthetics clinic) are unverified first-party claims, not independently audited.

Will callers know they're talking to an AI?

Macaws' own FAQ states its AI receptionists use "ultra-realistic voices" trained for natural conversation, and that "most callers assume they are speaking to a human," while also recommending natural disclosure in the call greeting. Depending on your jurisdiction, disclosing that callers are speaking with an AI may be a legal requirement, not just a courtesy — check local rules before deploying this without a greeting disclosure.
