Our Verdict

Handoff is an AI estimating and job-management platform for US contractors: remodelers, handymen, kitchen and bath, painters, roofers, electricians, plumbers and more. You describe the job by voice, notes, photos or uploaded drawings, and Handoff builds an itemised estimate using localised material and labour costs that it says update daily for every US city. From there it generates branded proposals, material lists, digital signatures, invoices and online payments, with QuickBooks Online, Google Calendar, MagicPlan and Zapier integrations. Flex costs $149 a month ($119 billed annually) with 50 AI credits, one pricing catalog and 2 users; Pro costs $299 a month ($239 billed annually, 12-month commitment) with unlimited AI credits, 5 users, client financing, AI site walkthroughs, project management and a dedicated success coach; Scale ($899, or $719 billed annually) adds AI takeoffs from residential plans up to 7,000 sq ft, a subcontractor portal and API/MCP access. The trade-offs: it is built for the US market, Pro and Scale lock you into a 12-month commitment, Flex's 50 monthly AI credits can run out for busy estimators, and AI numbers still need a contractor's review before they go to a client. For a small remodeling or trade business that loses evenings to estimates, the no-card 7-day trial is an easy way to check its numbers against your own. This verdict is built from Handoff's own published pages — RankerToolAI has not run a hands-on lab test of Handoff.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Handoff AI free?

Handoff offers a 7-day free trial with no credit card required. After that, plans start at $119/mo billed annually.

How much does Handoff AI cost?

Flex is $149/mo ($119 billed annually), Pro $299/mo ($239 billed annually) and Scale $899/mo ($719 billed annually). Pro and Scale need a 12-month commitment.

Where does Handoff get its cost data?

Handoff says its material and labour rates come from supplier catalog integrations and national labour-rate tracking, updated daily and localised to any US location.

Which trades can use Handoff?

Remodelers, handymen, general contractors, kitchen and bath, painters, drywall, electrical, plumbing, HVAC, roofing, concrete, landscaping and more.

Does Handoff work outside the US?

Its cost data is localised for US locations, so it is designed primarily for US contractors.
