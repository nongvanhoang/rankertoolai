Our Verdict

Signitic is a French email signature manager for companies on Google Workspace or Microsoft 365. It connects to your Google directory or Microsoft Entra ID, so signatures update automatically when someone joins, leaves or changes role, and they work in Gmail, Outlook, iOS and Android. Marketing can add clickable banners to every signature for campaigns and events, with Canva import, audience targeting and A/B tests, while access rights let marketing own the templates and IT own the permissions. It integrates with HubSpot, Salesforce and Marketo, and the company states ISO 27001 certification and EU-only data hosting under GDPR. The free plan covers up to 19 employees with 1 template and 1 banner campaign, but shows a Signitic logo. Standard costs €1 per user per month excluding VAT, with a 20-licence minimum, and adds unlimited templates and campaigns, segmentation, A/B testing and statistics. The trade-offs: the minimum means a 20-person bill even for a smaller team, prices exclude VAT, it only supports Google Workspace and Microsoft 365, and digital business cards (vCards) and HRIS integrations sit on the custom plan.
