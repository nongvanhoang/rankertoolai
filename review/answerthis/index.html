Our Verdict

AnswerThis is built for the slowest part of academic work: finding the right papers and turning them into a properly cited literature review. You ask a research question, and it searches a database of 250M+ academic papers (the homepage cites 300M+), returns cited answers and summaries, and lets you organise sources into projects and a personal library that export to Zotero, Mendeley or EndNote. The paid tiers go much further, with an AI Writer for drafting manuscripts in 2,000+ citation styles, a Canvas for revising drafts, and an agent-led systematic review workflow that follows PRISMA. Its most important design choice for academic use is that citations are checked against the source material and unsourced claims are removed rather than invented. On the downside, Pro at $35/mo is pricier than general chatbots, the pricing page doesn't spell out the free plan's search limits, and like any AI research tool its summaries still need checking against the actual papers before you cite them. This verdict is built from AnswerThis's own published pages — RankerToolAI has not run a hands-on lab test of AnswerThis.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is AnswerThis free?

Yes. The free plan needs no credit card and includes cited search across 250M+ papers, summaries, 5 saved projects and export to reference managers. Pro costs $35/mo.

Does AnswerThis make up citations?

According to AnswerThis, citations are verified against source material and unsourced claims are removed. You should still check key claims against the original papers before citing them.

Does AnswerThis work with Zotero or Mendeley?

Yes. Even the free plan exports to Zotero, Mendeley and EndNote.

Can AnswerThis do a systematic review?

Pro includes an agent-led systematic review workflow following PRISMA. Lab & Team adds dual-reviewer screening, meta-analysis and evidence grading.

Is there a plan for a research group or university?

Yes. Lab & Team is $75 per seat per month billed annually (minimum 3 seats), and Max is a $250/mo campus-wide site licence with SSO and LMS integration.
