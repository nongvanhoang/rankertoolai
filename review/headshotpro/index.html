What HeadshotPro Offers

How It Works: Upload, Select, Download

The whole process is 3 steps: upload a handful of selfies from your phone or laptop, choose from thousands of backdrop/outfit style combinations (or use the "Choose for me" AI pairing if you'd rather not pick), and download 30-70 headshots as JPEGs — typically ready in under 30 minutes. An auto-validation step flags problematic source photos (bad lighting, obscured face) before generation starts, which cuts down on wasted credits from unusable input.

Editing & LinkedIn-Ready Cropping

Every package includes edit credits to swap outfits, change backdrops, or remove blemishes on generated photos after the fact, rather than starting over from scratch. A built-in profile picture editor handles platform-specific cropping — most notably LinkedIn's preferred headshot framing — so you're not manually resizing in a separate tool afterward.

Team & Enterprise Features

For companies ordering headshots across a team, HeadshotPro uses a credit system (1 credit per person) with volume discounts that scale from 20% off at 6+ credits up to 60% off at 200+ credits, plus a team admin interface with passwordless login for each employee. This is the feature set that's landed HeadshotPro with Fortune 500 clients like HubSpot and Solugenix — coordinating one AI headshot session per employee is dramatically simpler than scheduling an in-person photographer for an entire company.

Guarantee & Real Refund Data

HeadshotPro's guarantee is "100% money back, no expiry" if you don't walk away with at least one profile-worthy headshot. Frequently Asked Questions

Is HeadshotPro free?

No, HeadshotPro has no permanent free plan — the Starter pack starts at $29 for 30 headshots. There's no subscription, though: it's a one-time purchase and the credits never expire. HeadshotPro backs every purchase with a 100% money-back guarantee if you don't get at least one profile-worthy headshot.

How much does HeadshotPro cost?

Individual packages are one-time purchases: Starter is $29 for 30 headshots, and Professional is $59 for 70 headshots with an optional 4K upscale. Teams use a credit system (1 credit per person) with volume discounts — 20% off at 6+ credits, 30% at 10+, up to 60% off at 200+ credits — and custom invoicing is available for enterprise.

Is HeadshotPro worth it?

For most people who need a professional headshot without booking a photographer, yes. At $29-59 as a one-time purchase, it's far cheaper than a studio session, delivers 30-70 options in under 30 minutes, and the guarantee has a genuinely low real-world refund rate (3.3% of 217,879 purchases 2023-2026). The main caveat: a portion of Trustpilot reviewers report the AI output doesn't always closely resemble them — result quality depends on the input selfies you upload.

What is HeadshotPro best for?

HeadshotPro is best for individuals who need a LinkedIn or resume-ready headshot fast and cheaply, and for teams/companies that need consistent-looking staff headshots without coordinating everyone's schedules for an in-person shoot — it's used by companies including HubSpot and Solugenix for exactly that. It's less suited to anyone who specifically needs a real, unedited photograph (e.g. for a government ID).
