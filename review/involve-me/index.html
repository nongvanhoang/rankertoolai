Our Verdict

involve.me builds the interactive pages that sit between an ad and a sales call: quizzes, assessments, quote and ROI calculators, product finders, booking funnels, forms and landing pages. You can start from a template or describe what you want to its AI agent, which builds the steps, fields, conditional logic, copy, design and follow-up emails. Leads land in a free built-in CRM with no contact limit, email sequences branch on answers and scores, and payments run through Stripe, PayPal or Square inside the funnel. It has 50+ native integrations, including HubSpot, Salesforce, ActiveCampaign, Brevo, Mailchimp, Klaviyo and Pipedrive, plus Zapier and webhooks. The free plan allows 50 submissions a month. Start costs $29/mo billed annually ($49 monthly) for 3 live funnels and 1 user, Grow $69/mo ($99) for 5 funnels and 2 users, and Scale $139/mo ($199) for 25 funnels and 5 users. The trade-offs: live-funnel limits are tight on the lower plans, monthly billing costs much more than annual, and AI use runs on monthly credits. This verdict is built from involve.me's own published pages — RankerToolAI has not run a hands-on test of involve.me.
