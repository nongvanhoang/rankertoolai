Our Verdict

Team Pulse combines project management and a CRM so a small agency or service team can win a deal and run the work in the same place. Projects use Kanban boards with subtasks, due dates and priorities; each project has its own chat, comments and files; and the CRM tracks deals, revenue, win rates and team performance in a visual pipeline with leaderboards. AI features include an AI Assist chat, workflow generation and project-level insights, metered by monthly AI credits. Every plan, including Free, allows unlimited collaborators, so you pay by projects and pipelines rather than seats. Free covers 2 projects; Starter is $29/mo for 5 projects but no pipeline; Growth is $79/mo for 10 projects and 1 pipeline; Pro $159/mo for 20 projects and 2 pipelines; Scale $299/mo for 40 projects and 4 pipelines, with paid plans billed annually. The trade-offs: the project caps are low for busy teams, the sales pipeline only starts on Growth, the site doesn't say how long the paid trial lasts, and only a few integrations such as Stripe are mentioned. This verdict is built from Team Pulse's own published pages — RankerToolAI has not run a hands-on test of Team Pulse.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Team Pulse free?

Yes. The free plan covers 2 projects with unlimited collaborators, chat, tasks and files, and needs no card.

How much does Team Pulse cost?

Starter is $29/mo, Growth $79/mo, Pro $159/mo and Scale $299/mo, billed annually.

Does Team Pulse include a CRM?

Yes, from the Growth plan, which adds 1 sales pipeline. Pro has 2 and Scale has 4.

Does Team Pulse charge per user?

No. Every plan includes unlimited collaborators; plans differ by projects, pipelines, AI credits and storage.
