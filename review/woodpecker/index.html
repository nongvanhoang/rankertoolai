Our Verdict

Woodpecker is a cold email tool built around deliverability. Instead of charging per seat or per mailbox, it charges for the number of prospects you contact each month, starting at $7 per 100 contacted prospects on monthly billing, with 33% off on annual billing. That price includes unlimited email verification, warm-up, unlimited team members and unlimited email accounts, which is generous compared with tools that charge per mailbox. Adaptive sending, inbox rotation, a centralized inbox and a domain audit help keep emails out of spam. LinkedIn outreach, extra warm-ups, API and integrations, and the agency panel are paid add-ons, so the real monthly cost depends on what you switch on. The 14-day trial (or 100 emails) needs no credit card. This verdict is built from Woodpecker's own published pages — RankerToolAI has not run a hands-on lab test of Woodpecker.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Woodpecker free?

Woodpecker has a 14-day free trial (or 100 cold emails, whichever comes first) with all features and no credit card. After that it's a paid tool.

How is Woodpecker priced?

By the number of prospects you contact per month, starting at $7 per 100 contacted prospects on monthly billing. Annual billing saves 33%.

Does Woodpecker include email warm-up?

Yes. Warm-up and unlimited catch-all email verification are included; extra warm-up slots cost $5/mo per email account.

Can Woodpecker send LinkedIn messages?

Yes, as a paid add-on at $29/mo per connected LinkedIn account.
