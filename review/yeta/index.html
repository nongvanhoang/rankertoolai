Our Verdict

Yeta AI does one thing: you paste a public YouTube link into its web player, pick a language, and it plays an AI-dubbed voice track in sync with the video, generating the audio segment by segment as you watch. For anyone who learns from YouTube in a language they only half understand — lectures, tutorials, courses — listening instead of reading subtitles is a genuinely more comfortable way to take in a long video, and it works while you're cooking or commuting. The pricing is simple and cheap: 15 free minutes every month with no card, or $7/mo for up to one hour of dubbing a day. The limits are just as clear: YouTube only, public videos only (no private or age-restricted ones), 10+ languages rather than dozens, and a single-purpose web player rather than a browser extension or a creator tool for publishing dubbed videos. This verdict is built from Yeta AI's own published pages — RankerToolAI has not run a hands-on lab test of Yeta AI.

Best Yeta AI Alternatives

Yeta AI isn't the only option in this space. Here's how the leading alternatives compare:

  • YouTube's own auto-translated captions — Free and built in, but you're reading subtitles rather than listening — the exact thing Yeta is designed to replace.
  • YouTube auto-dubbing — Some creators enable YouTube's own multi-language audio tracks, but only on their channels and only in the languages they chose.
  • ElevenLabs Dubbing — Much more powerful and multilingual, aimed at creators dubbing their own content, at a higher price. See our ElevenLabs review.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Yeta AI free?

Yes, there's a permanent free plan with 15 minutes of dubbing every month, no credit card and no trial period. The paid Unlimited plan is $7/mo for up to one hour a day.

Does Yeta AI work on any YouTube video?

It works on any public YouTube URL. Private and age-restricted videos aren't supported.

Which languages does Yeta AI support?

10+ languages, including English, Russian, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Chinese and Arabic.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Yeta AI plays videos in its own web player in the browser — no app, extension or download.

Who is Yeta AI best for?

Students and self-learners who watch long lectures, courses and tutorials in a language they don't speak fluently, and anyone who'd rather listen than read subtitles.
