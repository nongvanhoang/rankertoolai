Our Verdict

RankBolt automates a blog: it researches keywords and topic clusters for your market, writes the articles with AI images and internal and external links, and publishes them on a schedule to WordPress, Webflow, Shopify or Notion, with a REST API and webhooks for anything else. It writes in 40+ languages, tracks your Google rankings, and checks prompts daily in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude to see whether AI assistants mention you. Starter costs 69 CHF/mo for 1 site, 10 articles a month, 25 keywords checked weekly and 20 AI prompts; Pro is 149 CHF/mo for 3 sites, 30 articles, daily rank checks and 75 prompts; Agency is 299 CHF/mo for 10 sites and 60 articles. The 3-day trial needs no card. The trade-offs: Google's spam policies target large amounts of content published mainly to rank, so every article needs a human edit before it goes live; prices are in Swiss francs, so the cost moves with exchange rates; and 3 days is too short to judge results.
