Our Verdict

ViralityAI's pitch is analyzing your video before you post it — a viral score from 0-100 with a 10-parameter breakdown (hook strength, retention curve, CTA power, and more) rather than just guessing and checking analytics after the fact. No tool can guarantee virality, and that's not really the claim here; the more honest framing is that it's a structured pre-flight checklist backed by pattern analysis across trending content on TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube. The free plan (100 searches/month, 4 viral ideas) is generous enough to genuinely evaluate the discovery side before paying, and the $5.99/mo annual Pro tier is inexpensive for unlimited searches plus 20 video analyses/month. This verdict is built from ViralityAI's own published pricing and feature pages — RankerToolAI has not run a hands-on lab test of ViralityAI.
