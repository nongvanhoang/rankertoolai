Our Verdict

Robofy builds AI agents that talk to your customers on your website and on WhatsApp. A Support Agent answers questions from your own knowledge base, a Lead Agent qualifies visitors and sends them to your CRM or a Google Sheet, and an Action Agent can check order status, process cancellations or run other workflows through your API. When the AI can't help, it hands the conversation to a human with the context attached. Its strongest point is WhatsApp: a shared inbox, broadcast campaigns, message templates for Meta approval and consent management are built in, which many website-only chatbots don't offer. There's also a Voice AI phone agent and plugins for WordPress and WooCommerce. The trade-offs: plans are priced in AI credits (an AI message uses 60 credits, a voice minute 600), so Starter's 60,000 credits work out to roughly 1,000 messages a month; the pricing page lists two different agent limits for the same plans; and the jump from Starter at $19 to Pro at $99 is steep. This verdict is built from Robofy's own published pages — RankerToolAI has not run a hands-on lab test of Robofy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Robofy free?

There's no free plan, but there's a 14-day free trial with 2 agents, 5 web pages and 4,500 AI credits. Starter costs $19/mo after that.

Does Robofy work with WhatsApp?

Yes. WhatsApp Business is a core channel, with a shared inbox, broadcast campaigns, template management and consent management.

How many messages do I get on Robofy?

Robofy prices in credits. One AI message uses 60 credits, so Starter's 60,000 credits are about 1,000 messages a month and Pro's 1,000,000 are about 16,000.

Can Robofy hand a chat over to a human?

Yes. Human handoff passes the conversation, with context, to your team when the AI can't resolve it.

Does Robofy work with WordPress or WooCommerce?

Yes. Robofy has WordPress and WooCommerce plugins, plus an API, webhooks and Zapier on Pro.
