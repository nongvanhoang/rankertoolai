Our Verdict

SkySnail does one job: turning a video idea into a click-worthy thumbnail. You describe the video, upload the video or its transcript, or paste a YouTube link, and it generates thumbnails sized for YouTube, Reels, TikTok and Shorts. The features that matter most for a channel are the reusable face avatars (upload your face once and it appears in every thumbnail), a brand kit for your fonts, colours and logo, and custom templates built from a reference image, so a channel's thumbnails can stay consistent. A text-prompt editor handles tweaks, and transcript analysis suggests titles. The trade-offs: there is no free plan, only a 3-day trial with 5 credits that asks for a card; the entry plan's 50 credits a month and single avatar slot are tight for a channel that tests several thumbnails per video; and SkySnail doesn't publish a refund policy on its homepage. Its “17,907 users” figure is the company's own. This verdict is built from SkySnail's own published pages — RankerToolAI has not run a hands-on lab test of SkySnail.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is SkySnail free?

No. There's no free plan, but every plan starts with a 3-day free trial with 5 credits. SkySnail says your card isn't charged until the trial ends, and you can cancel anytime.

How much does SkySnail cost?

Creator Lite is $11/mo for 50 credits, Creator is $19/mo for 150 credits, and Creator Premium is $33/mo for 450 credits. Annual billing is cheaper.

Can SkySnail put my face on thumbnails?

Yes. You upload your face as an avatar once and reuse it. Lite has 1 avatar slot, Creator has 3, and Premium is unlimited.

Which platforms does SkySnail support?

YouTube, Instagram Reels, TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn, X and Threads, with export sizes for YouTube, Reels, TikTok and Shorts.

Do SkySnail thumbnails have a watermark?

No. All paid plans export without a watermark.
