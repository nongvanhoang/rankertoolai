Our Verdict

Key Features We Reviewed

Avatar Realism

HeyGen's current avatars focus on looking less robotic. The main improvements HeyGen highlights are: micro-expression variation (subtle eyebrow movements, slight squints, natural blinking patterns), realistic head movement (gentle nodding, slight position shifts between sentences), and improved eye contact (the avatar appears to look directly at the viewer without the fixed stare of earlier AI avatars).

How realistic an avatar looks depends a lot on the script, the voice and the viewer. Run one of your own scripts through the free plan and watch it at normal speed, the way your audience will.

Video Translation

The Video Translation feature is HeyGen's most unique capability. Upload a video of a real person speaking (or a HeyGen avatar), choose a target language, and HeyGen generates a new version where the person's lips are synchronized to the translated audio. The result is a video where the person appears to be speaking the target language.

We reviewed Video Translation on a 5-minute English product video, converting it to Spanish, French, German, Japanese, and Hindi. Spanish and French results were excellent — sync accuracy was near-perfect and native speakers rated the audio quality as "good" to "very good." German was slightly off on longer compound words. Japanese and Hindi were functional but showed more artifacts. For European language localization, Video Translation is a commercial-ready solution.

Instant Avatar

HeyGen's Instant Avatar requires only a 2-minute video recording of yourself to create a personal avatar. Unlike Synthesia's Personal Avatar (which requires 30 minutes), the Instant Avatar trains in minutes. Quality is lower than Synthesia's longer-trained avatar — more artifacts appear in complex facial movements — but for quick personalization, it's significantly more accessible. A 5-minute video training produces noticeably better results.

StreamingAvatar API

HeyGen's StreamingAvatar API allows real-time interactive avatar applications — think AI-powered customer service representatives or live avatar-presented content. We reviewed it with a simple chatbot integration: latency was 1-2 seconds from text input to avatar response, which is acceptable for interactive applications. This is a capability Synthesia doesn't offer and represents HeyGen's strongest differentiator for developer use cases.

Template Library

HeyGen's template library covers the most common use cases well. Social media formats (Shorts, Reels, TikTok), product explainers, and sales videos are well-represented. The editor follows a simple script-to-video flow, so most people can finish a first video without a tutorial.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is HeyGen used for?

HeyGen is used to create AI avatar videos where a digital human presenter delivers your script without any recording equipment. Common use cases include product explainers, sales videos, social media content, corporate training, and multilingual video localization via Video Translation.

How realistic are HeyGen's avatars?

HeyGen's avatars are among the most realistic in the category, with natural head movement, blinking and eye contact. Realism is subjective, so the best check is the free plan: it gives 3 videos a month (up to 1 minute each) to judge with your own script.

What is HeyGen Video Translation?

HeyGen Video Translation takes an existing video of a real person speaking, transcribes it, translates it, and generates a lip-synced version where the person appears to speak the new language. Quality varies by language, so test your target language before relying on it.

How does HeyGen compare to Synthesia?

HeyGen is stronger for marketing and creator video, with Video Translation and long videos on its credit plans. Synthesia is stronger for training: a minute-based plan, a free plan with about 10 minutes a month, and SCORM export on Enterprise. For individual creators and agencies, start with HeyGen. For L&D teams and enterprise training, start with Synthesia.

How much does HeyGen cost?

HeyGen has a free plan (3 videos/month, up to 1 minute each), Creator at $29/month (about $24 billed yearly, 600 credits), Pro at $49/month (about $41 yearly, 1,000 credits, 4K), Business at $149/month plus $20 per seat, and custom Enterprise pricing, as listed on heygen.com in October 2026. The free plan is a genuine trial for checking quality before paying.

Can HeyGen translate existing videos?

Yes. HeyGen's Video Translation feature is one of its best features. Upload a video, choose the target language, and HeyGen generates a version where the person's lips are synced to the translated audio. Check which plan includes the languages and video length you need on HeyGen's pricing page.
