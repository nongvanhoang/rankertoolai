What Each Tool Actually Does

Dropmagic is an AI store builder. You paste a product link from AliExpress, Amazon, Alibaba, Etsy, Teemdrop or another Shopify store, and it generates a complete branded store. The AI writes the headline, benefits and objection-handling copy, applies your brand kit, turns supplier photos into lifestyle images, and assembles mobile-first pages from 100+ sections, with bundles and a side cart included. It then publishes straight to Shopify, with no code and no app blocks. It launched on the Shopify App Store in June 2025 and rates 4.9/5 there from 49 reviews.

PagePilot is an AI product page builder. Instead of a whole store, it produces individual conversion-focused landing pages that slot into a Shopify store you already run, and it works with all Shopify themes. Every plan includes 8+ templates, 35+ sections and blocks, 30+ languages, AI text and image editing, a customisable cart drawer, and upsells. It also rates around 4.9/5 on the Shopify App Store, from a larger review base than Dropmagic.

Pricing Compared

Dropmagic keeps pricing simple. The Free plan lets you import a product and build and preview one store, but it can't publish. Pro costs $79/mo and adds unlimited stores, advanced AI generation, multilingual copy, premium templates, 30 AI image editor credits a month, a free .store domain, removal of Dropmagic branding, and priority support. It's billed monthly and can be cancelled anytime.

PagePilot has three tiers. Lite is $39/mo for 10 product pages, 50 AI images and 1 store. Starter is $59/mo for unlimited pages, 200 AI images and 3 stores. Scaler is $99/mo for unlimited pages and images across 5 stores. Paying yearly gives 2 months free, and you can build for free before paying to publish.

Here's the practical maths. If you run one or two stores, PagePilot's Starter plan ($59) gets you unlimited pages for $20/mo less than Dropmagic. Dropmagic only becomes the cheaper option once you run more than five stores, because PagePilot's top plan caps out at 5.

Store Builder vs Page Builder: Why It Matters

The two tools look similar in demos, and both promise that you paste a link and get a page. The difference lies in what surrounds that page. Dropmagic generates the store around the product: brand kit, multiple pages and a consistent design. That's what you want when you're testing a new niche and don't have a store yet. PagePilot assumes the store already exists and concentrates on the page a paid-traffic visitor lands on, with upsells and a cart drawer to lift order value.

If you're running ads to a store that already converts, rebuilding the whole store around every product is overkill, and PagePilot's lighter approach fits. If you're launching your first store, or a new store for every product test, PagePilot leaves you to sort out the rest of the store yourself, and Dropmagic does that part for you.

What Neither Tool Does

Neither tool picks the product for you, sources it, or fulfils orders. A beautiful page won't save a product nobody wants or a supplier that ships in four weeks. For that side of the business, look at a sourcing tool such as Syncee, or see our full roundup of the best AI tools for Shopify dropshipping.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Dropmagic cheaper than PagePilot?

No, not to start. PagePilot's plans start at $39/mo (10 product pages) and $59/mo (unlimited pages, 3 stores), while Dropmagic's only paid plan is $79/mo. Dropmagic's $79 does include unlimited stores, which works out cheaper if you run more than 5 stores (PagePilot's top plan, $99/mo, covers 5).

Can I use Dropmagic or PagePilot for free?

Both let you build for free. Dropmagic's free plan builds and previews one store, and PagePilot lets you create pages without a credit card. Neither lets you publish to Shopify until you pay.

Which is better for a brand-new store?

Dropmagic. It builds a complete branded store from one product link, including copy, brand kit and lifestyle images. PagePilot is designed to add product pages to a store that already exists.

Which gives more AI images?

PagePilot. Its plans include 50, 200 or unlimited AI images a month, while Dropmagic Pro includes 30 AI image editor credits a month.

Do they work outside Shopify?

No. Both Dropmagic and PagePilot are Shopify-only.
