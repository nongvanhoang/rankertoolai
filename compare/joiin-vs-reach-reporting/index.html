Joiin vs Reach Reporting (2026): Consolidation or Full FP&A?

Independent guide by Hoang Nong · Updated November 28, 2026 · Based on each vendor's published pricing and feature pages, not a hands-on test. See our methodology.

Disclosure: this page contains affiliate links to Joiin. We have no affiliate deal with Reach Reporting. We may earn a commission if you buy through our links, at no extra cost to you. Read our disclosure.

Short Answer

Choose Joiin if the job is consolidating several companies into group reports: 2 companies cost $41 a month ($32 billed yearly) and 10 cost $122. Choose Reach Reporting if you also need 3-way forecasting, multi-year planning and client portals: plans start at $149 a month for 1 connection and $290 for 10. Joiin also connects to Sage and Zoho Books, which Reach does not list on its homepage.

Features

  • Consolidation: both do it. Joiin is built around it (chart of accounts mapping and eliminations on every plan). Reach lists multi-currency consolidation on every plan; on Joiin, multi-currency starts at Pro.
  • Forecasting: Reach's clear advantage. It lists a 3-way FP&A model, multi-year planning, and cash flow, AR, AP and inventory projections. Joiin covers budgets, but forecasting is not its focus.
  • Client delivery: Reach includes unlimited client portal access, branded live reports and shareable dashboards. Joiin produces report packs and dashboards with unlimited users and clients.
  • Data out: Joiin's Max plan adds an API, a Zapier connection and an MCP connection to Claude and ChatGPT. Reach lists a Google Sheets integration on every plan.
  • AI: Reach lists "AI-powered data storytelling". Joiin's AI features are covered in our review.

Integrations

Joiin's pricing page lists ten accounting connections, including Xero, QuickBooks, Sage, MYOB and Zoho Books, so the companies in a group can sit on different systems. Reach's homepage lists QuickBooks Online, Xero, Digits, MYOB, Google Sheets and Excel. If any company in your group runs Sage or Zoho Books, Joiin is the simpler fit.

Mixed groups are common. Our guide to consolidating Xero and QuickBooks together walks through one.

Who Should Choose Which

  • Choose Joiin if you are a group finance lead or an accountant who needs consolidated P&L, balance sheet and cash flow for several companies, at a price that scales gently with company count.
  • Choose Reach Reporting if you are an advisory firm that sells forecasting and planning to clients and wants branded portals, and the companies you report on sit on QuickBooks or Xero.
  • Use both only if you need Reach's forecasting for a few advisory clients and Joiin's low per-company price for many consolidation clients.

FAQ

Is Joiin cheaper than Reach Reporting?

Yes, by a wide margin for consolidation. Two companies cost $41 a month on Joiin (Core, billed monthly) against $290 on Reach's 10-connection plan, the cheapest Reach option that covers two connections.

Does Reach Reporting do consolidation?

Yes. Multi-currency consolidation is listed on every Reach plan, alongside 3-way forecasting, multi-year planning and cash flow projections.

Which one has a longer free trial?

Reach Reporting says it offers a 30-day free trial with no credit card. Joiin offers 14 days of its Max plan, also with no card.

Do they charge per user?

No. Both list unlimited users. Joiin charges by the number of companies; Reach charges by the number of connections.
