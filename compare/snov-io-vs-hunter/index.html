Snov.io vs Hunter (2026): Which Email Finder Gives You More?

Independent guide by Hoang Nong · Updated November 28, 2026 · Based on each vendor's published pricing and feature pages, not a hands-on test. See our methodology.

Disclosure: this page contains affiliate links to Snov.io. We have no affiliate deal with Hunter. We may earn a commission if you buy through our links, at no extra cost to you. Read our disclosure.

Short Answer

Both have a free plan with 50 credits a month. Hunter gives more credits per dollar on paper (2,000 credits for $49 a month against Snov.io's 1,000 for $39), so it suits teams that mostly find and verify emails in volume. Snov.io bundles email warm-up (3 slots on Starter, unlimited on Pro) and unlimited team seats, so it suits small teams that want finding, warming and sending in one plan. Credits are not defined the same way by both tools, so test both free plans on the same list.

Sending and Warm-Up

Both tools can send cold email sequences, but they package it differently. Snov.io counts recipients per month (5,000 on Starter, 25,000 on Pro, per our review) and includes warm-up for your sending mailboxes with deliverability checks. Hunter counts recipients per sequence (2,500 on Starter up to 15,000 on Scale) and caps how many email accounts you can connect.

If your domain is new, warm-up matters more than credit totals: sending from a cold mailbox is the fastest way into spam. Our guide on keeping cold emails out of spam covers SPF, DKIM and DMARC.

Who Should Choose Which

  • Choose Hunter if you mainly look up and verify emails in volume, for example for an agency or a research team, and send from a tool you already have.
  • Choose Snov.io if you are a small sales team that wants one tool to find leads, warm up mailboxes and run sequences, with every teammate on the same plan.
  • Choose either free plan if you send fewer than about a hundred emails a month.

How to Test Both for Free

  • Pick the same 50 company domains you actually want to reach.
  • Run them through both free plans and count how many emails each finds and marks as valid.
  • Send a small sequence from a warmed mailbox and compare replies and bounces, not opens.
  • Upgrade only the tool that found more usable emails for your market.

FAQ

Is Snov.io cheaper than Hunter?

At the entry level Snov.io is cheaper per month ($39 against $49) but Hunter includes twice the credits (2,000 against 1,000), so Hunter costs less per credit on paper.

Do Snov.io and Hunter have free plans?

Yes. Both give 50 credits a month for free. Hunter's free plan includes 1 connected email account and 500 recipients per sequence; Snov.io's includes 100 recipients and 1 warm-up slot.

Which one includes email warm-up?

Snov.io includes warm-up on every plan: 1 slot free, 3 on Starter and unlimited on Pro. Hunter's pricing page does not list warm-up in the plan limits we read.

Is a Snov.io credit the same as a Hunter credit?

Not necessarily. Each tool decides what an action costs in credits, so compare results on the same list of domains rather than credit totals alone.
