Insightful vs Hubstaff (2026)

Independent guide by Hoang Nong · Updated November 2026 · Based on each vendor's published pricing and feature pages, not a hands-on test. See our methodology.

Short Answer

Choose Insightful to understand how a salaried team spends its time: app and website usage, capacity and workload reports, with screenshots included, from $8 per seat a month billed yearly. Choose Hubstaff to track and pay hourly contractors: it adds timesheets, invoicing, payroll and, on Enterprise, GPS tracking, from $4.99 per seat a month billed yearly.

What Each Tool Is Built For

Insightful is a workforce analytics tool. Its Analytics plan covers time and attendance, app and website usage, capacity and workload reports, and screenshots. Insights (50 seats or more) adds People Intelligence reports on work patterns. The questions it answers are about the team: who is overloaded, where time goes, which tools people actually use.

Hubstaff is a time tracker that pays people. Every plan includes time tracking, timesheets, and clients and invoices. Grow adds reports, project budgets, expenses, idle timeout and one integration. Team adds unlimited screenshots and app and URL tracking, payments and payroll, and a 3-month trial of Hubstaff Insights. Enterprise adds GPS time and location tracking, work orders, geofencing, a Silent app, HIPAA compliance, SOC 2 Type II and SCIM provisioning.

Screenshots and Monitoring

If screenshots matter, the entry plans differ. Insightful includes screenshots on Analytics, its first plan. Hubstaff limits screenshots and app and URL tracking on Starter and Grow, and removes the limits from Team ($10 per seat billed yearly).

Monitoring rules differ by country. Tell your team what is tracked and why, and check local law before turning on screenshots or recording.

Cost for a 10-Person Team

Per year, billed yearly, before add-ons:

  • Hubstaff Starter: $598.80 (limited screenshots and app tracking)
  • Hubstaff Grow: $900
  • Insightful Analytics: $960 (screenshots included)
  • Hubstaff Team: $1,200 (unlimited screenshots, payroll)
  • Hubstaff Enterprise: $3,000 (GPS, Silent app, compliance)

Billed monthly, the same team pays $100 a month on Insightful Analytics, or $70, $90 or $120 a month on Hubstaff Starter, Grow or Team. For comparable monitoring (unlimited screenshots and app tracking), Insightful Analytics at $960 a year costs less than Hubstaff Team at $1,200. Hubstaff Team earns its price if you also run payroll through it.

Who Should Choose Which

  • Salaried remote or hybrid team, manager wants workload visibility: Insightful.
  • Agency billing clients by the hour: Hubstaff, for timesheets, invoices and project budgets.
  • Paying contractors through the tracker: Hubstaff Team or above, for payments and payroll.
  • Field staff on job sites: Hubstaff Enterprise, for GPS, work orders and geofencing.
  • Small team on a tight budget: Hubstaff Starter is the lowest price, but check the screenshot and tracking limits first.

FAQ

Is Insightful cheaper than Hubstaff?

Hubstaff's entry plan is cheaper ($4.99 against $8 per seat a month, billed yearly), but it limits screenshots and app tracking. For unlimited screenshots and app tracking, Insightful Analytics ($8) costs less than Hubstaff Team ($10).

Does Hubstaff have a free plan?

Hubstaff's pricing page offers a 14-day free trial with no credit card rather than a free plan, plus a 30-day money-back guarantee on paid plans. Insightful also offers a free trial without a card.

Which is better for payroll?

Hubstaff. Payments and payroll are included from its Team plan, and every plan has clients and invoices. Insightful focuses on analytics rather than paying people.

Does Insightful have GPS tracking?

Insightful's plans don't list GPS tracking. Hubstaff includes GPS time and location tracking, work orders and geofencing on its Enterprise plan.

Is there a seat minimum?

Hubstaff's Starter, Grow and Team plans have a 2-seat minimum. Insightful lists a 50-seat minimum on its Insights plan; check the minimum for Analytics when you sign up.

Which has better productivity analytics?

Insightful is built for it, with capacity and workload reports on its first plan and People Intelligence on Insights. Hubstaff offers Insights as part of Enterprise and as a 3-month trial on Team.
