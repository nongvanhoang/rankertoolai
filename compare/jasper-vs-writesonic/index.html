Writesonic is no longer a budget AI writing tool. What Each Tool Does Now

Jasper: an AI writing platform for marketing teams

Jasper is built around producing marketing content: its Canvas workspace, marketing agents, Brand Voices (2 on Pro), Knowledge assets and Audiences keep output consistent with your brand. The Business plan adds agents for more complex workflows (including GEO, translations and deep research), a no-code agent builder, API access and admin controls. If your job is writing blog posts, emails, ads and campaign assets, this is still the tool in this pair that does that.

Writesonic: an AI search visibility platform

Writesonic now monitors how often and how favorably a brand shows up in AI-generated answers, prioritizes the gaps, and suggests or executes fixes such as content updates and technical audits. Every plan bundles a limited number of AI articles and site audits, but those exist to support visibility work, not as a standalone writer. If you need to know whether ChatGPT or Google AI Overviews mention your brand, this is the tool in this pair built for that.

What's gone from Writesonic

The free plan, the low-cost writer plans, Chatsonic (its chatbot with web access) and the old template library are no longer part of what Writesonic sells. Older comparisons that recommend Writesonic as a cheap Jasper alternative are out of date.

FAQ

Is Jasper or Writesonic better?
They no longer do the same job. Jasper is an AI writing platform; Writesonic is now an AI search visibility platform. Pick Jasper to create marketing content and Writesonic to track and improve how your brand appears in AI answers.
Is Writesonic still cheaper than Jasper?
No. Writesonic starts at $79/month (billed yearly) with no free plan. Jasper Pro starts at $59/month billed yearly ($69 monthly) with a 7-day free trial.
Does Writesonic still have an AI writer?
Only as a capped feature inside its visibility plans: 15 AI articles per month on Starter, up to 50 on Growth. The old free plan, low-cost writer plans and Chatsonic are no longer offered.
Does Jasper track AI search visibility?
Jasper's Business plan (custom pricing) includes agents for GEO work, but tracking brand mentions across AI answers is Writesonic's core product, available from its self-serve plans.
