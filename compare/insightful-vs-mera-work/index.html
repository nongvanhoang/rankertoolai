Features

Insightful is built around workforce analytics. The Analytics plan covers a real-time overview, capacity analysis (who is overloaded, who has room), app and website usage, and screenshots. Insights adds reports on work patterns and workload balance for 50 or more seats, and Intelligence adds process maps. Insider-threat detection, audit logs and SSO come with the $4 workspace security add-on.

Mera Work is built around simple monitoring. Every account gets a live dashboard, activity monitoring, app and website tracking, periodic screenshots, live screen streaming, attendance and automatic time tracking, plus SSO, role-based access and GPO deployment for IT teams. It also offers stealth mode and keyboard and mouse activity tracking, which need a clear policy with employees (see below).

Data Retention: the Real Difference

This is the one difference most buyers miss. Mera Work keeps data for up to three months. That is enough to check who worked when last week or last month, but not enough to compare this quarter with the same quarter last year, spot seasonal workload patterns or back up a performance review that covers a full year.

Insightful keeps 24 months, so year-over-year comparisons and long-running capacity trends are possible inside the tool. If you think you will only ever look at the last few weeks, Mera Work's shorter window will not hurt you, and you save money. If managers or HR will want history, pick Insightful or plan to export Mera Work reports regularly.

Privacy and Employee Trust

Both tools can record screens and activity, so the way you roll them out matters more than the brand. Tell your team what is tracked, why and who can see it; track work devices and work hours only; and prefer aggregate reports over constant screenshots. Mera Work's stealth mode and keyboard activity tracking in particular should only be used with a written policy and a check of local law. In the UK and EU, GDPR expects a clear purpose and proportionate monitoring, and some US states require written notice.

FAQ

Is Mera Work cheaper than Insightful?

Yes. Mera Work lists $3 per user per month billed annually ($4 monthly) with every feature included. Insightful starts at $8 per seat per month billed annually, and some features such as screen recording are $4 add-ons. For 10 people that is about $30 versus $80 a month.

How long do Insightful and Mera Work keep data?

Insightful keeps 24 months of data. Mera Work keeps data for up to three months, according to its pricing page.

Do Insightful and Mera Work have free trials?

Insightful has a free trial of its Analytics plan with no credit card, plus a demo. Mera Work has a 14-day free trial with every feature and no card.

Which is better for a small business?

For a very small team on a tight budget that only needs day-to-day visibility, Mera Work is the cheaper fit. Once you manage around 10 or more people and want workload and capacity reports or history beyond three months, Insightful is the stronger choice.

Can employees see that they are being monitored?

That depends on how you configure each tool and on local law. Mera Work offers a stealth mode, but in many places you must tell employees about monitoring. The safest approach with either tool is to publish a clear monitoring policy before rollout.
