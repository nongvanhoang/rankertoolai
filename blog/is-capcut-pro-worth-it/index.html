Is CapCut Pro Worth It? Honest Look at Pricing, AI Tools & Cancellation (2026)

CapCut's free tier is genuinely good — so what does Pro actually add, is it worth ~$19.99/month, and why does this app sit at 1.2/5 on Trustpilot despite being one of the most-downloaded editors around? Here's the honest, research-based breakdown.

What You Already Get for Free

CapCut's free tier is not a stripped-down demo — it's a fully capable editor across mobile, desktop, web, and iPad, with projects syncing between devices. Critically, exports don't carry a forced watermark, which is the single biggest reason free-tier CapCut is worth using on its own: many competing "free" editors gate watermark removal behind a paywall, CapCut doesn't. The free tier also includes CapCut's core AI features — auto-captions, background removal, and a large template library that leans into trending social formats.

What Pro Actually Adds

CapCut Pro (roughly $19.99/month or ~$179.99/year, per independent pricing trackers — CapCut does not publish one fixed global price) layers three things on top of the free tier: expanded AI generation credits for tools like Dreamina and the Seedance video/image models, 100GB of cloud storage (confirmed via CapCut's own help pages), and access to premium templates and effects. A 7-day free trial lets you test these before being charged.

Whether that's worth ~$20/month comes down to one question: do you actually hit the free tier's AI-credit or storage limits in a normal month? If you're an occasional editor who mostly uses templates and basic cuts, you likely never will. If you're generating AI clips or images regularly and burning through credits fast, Pro's expanded allowance can genuinely pay for itself in time saved.

⚠ The real reason CapCut sits at 1.2/5 on Trustpilot

CapCut's Trustpilot score (1,221 reviews, roughly 87% 1-star) is dragged down almost entirely by one recurring theme: getting charged when a free trial ends, struggling to find the cancel option, and being billed after already canceling. It is not primarily about editing quality or crashes — read a sample of the actual 1-star reviews and the billing pattern is specific and repeated enough to treat as a real risk, not review-platform noise.

How to Cancel CapCut Pro Without Getting Charged Again

Based on the pattern described across independent reviews, here's the safest way to avoid becoming one of them:

  1. Cancel directly inside CapCut's account/subscription settings — not by deleting the app, which does not stop billing on its own.
  2. Do this well before your renewal date, not on the day itself — give the cancellation time to process.
  3. Take a screenshot of the cancellation confirmation screen or email as a record.
  4. Check your card or app-store statement for the next 1-2 billing cycles to confirm no further charge went through.
  5. If a charge does appear after cancellation, dispute it with your card issuer or app store (Apple/Google) directly rather than relying solely on CapCut support, since slow support response is itself a recurring complaint.

FAQs

Is CapCut Pro worth paying for?

Only if you'll actually use the extra AI generation credits, 100GB of cloud storage, or premium templates every month. If you mainly need a free, no-watermark editor, the free tier already covers that — Pro adds nothing you need.

Why does CapCut have such bad reviews if the app is free and popular?

CapCut's 1.2/5 Trustpilot score (1,221 reviews, ~87% 1-star) is driven almost entirely by Pro subscription billing complaints — being charged after a free trial, difficulty finding the cancel button, and being billed after canceling — not by the free editor's actual quality.

How do I cancel CapCut Pro so it doesn't charge me again?

Cancel inside the app or web account's subscription settings well before your renewal date (not by deleting the app, which does not cancel billing), take a screenshot of the cancellation confirmation, and check your card statement for the next 1-2 billing cycles to confirm no further charge went through.

Does CapCut charge you automatically after the free trial?

Yes — like most app free trials, CapCut Pro's 7-day trial converts to a paid subscription automatically unless you cancel before it ends. This auto-conversion is the single most common theme in CapCut's negative reviews, so set a reminder before starting any trial.
