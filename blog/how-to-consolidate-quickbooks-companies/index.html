Why QuickBooks Online Can't Do It on Its Own

Each QuickBooks Online subscription is one company with its own chart of accounts, and its reports only run inside that company. Answers on Intuit's own community forum say it plainly: QuickBooks permits one company per subscription and the standard plans have no feature to consolidate transactions and reports across companies.

Classes and locations don't fix this. They split one company's figures by department or branch. If your group has separate legal entities, each with its own balance sheet and tax registration, they need separate company files, and the consolidation has to happen somewhere else.

Intuit Enterprise Suite

Intuit Enterprise Suite is Intuit's multi-entity product. Intuit's help article lists consolidated reports including a Consolidated Profit and Loss, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow Statement and Trial Balance, plus consolidated A/R and A/P aging, sales by customer and transaction reports. Users with access to two or more companies can filter the consolidated view by company, shared dimension or transaction origin.

Intuit also documents multi-entity eliminations and journal entries, intercompany transactions (marked "IC" in reports) and mapping charts of accounts by name or through a shared chart of accounts. If your group is outgrowing QuickBooks Online and you want everything in one Intuit system, start here. Pricing is not shown on those help pages, so get a quote from Intuit before comparing it with the options below.

QuickBooks Desktop Enterprise

On the desktop side, Intuit's multi-company page says Desktop Enterprise can manage multiple companies from a single dashboard with combined reporting and intercompany transaction tracking. Three conditions apply, per that page:

  • To consolidate reports from multiple company files, the files must all be on the same version of QuickBooks Desktop Enterprise.
  • The feature requires Microsoft Office 2013-2019 or Microsoft 365.
  • Intercompany transactions are only available on the Platinum and Diamond subscriptions.

Intuit also notes Desktop Enterprise is not available outside the U.S. If you run QuickBooks Pro or Premier, or a subsidiary sits outside the U.S., use one of the next two methods.

The Spreadsheet Method

For two companies with similar charts of accounts, a spreadsheet works:

  1. In each company, run the Trial Balance report for the same period end and accounting method (accrual or cash).
  2. Export each report to Excel and paste it into its own tab.
  3. Map every account to one group chart of accounts with a lookup column.
  4. Add an eliminations column that reverses intercompany sales, management fees, loans and balances.
  5. Sum the mapped columns plus eliminations to get the consolidated trial balance, then build the P&L and balance sheet from it and check the balance sheet still balances.

Our free consolidation Excel template sets this up for you, with eliminations and balance checks built in. The problem is upkeep: every new account breaks the mapping, every month needs fresh exports, and eliminations are typed in by hand. Once you have three or more companies, a foreign subsidiary or a board that wants a monthly pack, the spreadsheet usually costs more in time than an app costs in fees.

FAQ

Can QuickBooks Online consolidate multiple companies?

Not in the standard plans. Each QuickBooks Online subscription covers one company, and its reports run inside that company. Intuit Enterprise Suite, Intuit's multi-entity product, adds consolidated reports such as a consolidated P&L, balance sheet, cash flow and trial balance. On other plans, groups combine reports in a spreadsheet or connect the companies to a consolidation app such as Joiin.

Can QuickBooks Desktop combine reports from multiple company files?

QuickBooks Desktop Enterprise can combine reports from multiple company files, according to Intuit's multi-company page. The files must be on the same version of Desktop Enterprise and the feature needs Microsoft Office 2013-2019 or Microsoft 365. Intercompany transactions are only available on the Platinum and Diamond subscriptions.

Does Joiin work with QuickBooks?
