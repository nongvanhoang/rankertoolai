We Tracked Our Two Sites in ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI for 30 Days. Here's What Got Cited.

By Hoang Nong · November 27, 2026 · Data collected October 28 to November 24, 2026. See our methodology.

Disclosure: we used LLM Pulse on a free 30-day trial that its team activated for us, and we are an LLM Pulse affiliate. We paid nothing, and LLM Pulse had no say in this article. Read our disclosure.

Short Answer

For broad questions like "best AI video editor", AI assistants cited our sites almost never: 9 times in about 3,000 answers. When we asked narrow questions about specific products we had reviewed, our pages were cited in 8 of 75 answers within a day. In 30 days, no AI named either site in the text of an answer.

What we measured

We run two review sites: RankerToolAI (AI software) and RankerNest (consumer products). Each week, LLM Pulse asked the same questions to five AI systems: ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode. It then recorded every source each answer cited.

Who did get cited

On RankerToolAI's questions, the most-cited sources were YouTube (28% of answers), Reddit (24%) and Zapier (21%). On RankerNest's, google.com links led (30%), then YouTube (15%), Reddit (14%), Best Buy (12%) and the New York Times (10%). In share of voice among named review brands, Wirecutter took 62%.

The AI tool directories we set as competitors (Futurepedia, There's An AI For That, Toolify) were named only a handful of times. For AI tool questions, the real competition was YouTube creators, Reddit threads and Zapier's blog. We broke this down by AI system in where AI gets its AI tool recommendations.

Where our pages broke through

Our 9 citations from the main question set came from 6 pages, and every one covers a narrow topic:

  • RankerToolAI: our Shopify dropshipping tools comparison (4 times, all ChatGPT) and our Dropmagic review (once).
  • RankerNest: our dash cams under $300 guide (twice) and our DermSilk and North Edge reviews (once each).

So on the last day we added 15 narrow questions that matched pages we already had, such as "Joiin vs Spotlight Reporting" and "Rexing V1P Max review". Within hours, 8 of the 75 answers cited us. Our Joiin vs Spotlight Reporting comparison and our GearUP Booster alternatives page were the first source listed in Perplexity's answers.

That number flatters us. We picked those questions because we already had pages for them, and one run is a small sample. Still, the gap is wide: about 0.3% of answers on broad questions, about 11% on narrow ones.

What the AI searched for

ChatGPT shows the searches it runs before answering. It rarely searched our exact question. It added the year, "pricing", "features" and the names of rivals, for example: "GearUP Booster alternatives gaming network optimizer alternatives ExitLag LagoFast NoPing WTFast 2026". Pages that cover those rivals and show current prices match what the AI is actually looking for.

What we changed

  • We added the rivals the AI searched for (LagoFast and NoPing) to our GearUP Booster review.
  • We shortened the opening paragraph of our Joiin and GearUP reviews so the first lines answer the question, and we added a short sources list to each.
  • While checking sources, we found that our GearUP refund information was wrong. GearUP's user agreement makes purchases non-refundable by default, with refund rights listed for the EEA, Switzerland, the UK and South Korea. We corrected it on every page.

What we'd tell another small site

  1. Don't expect AI citations for broad "best X" questions while big publishers and YouTube own them.
  2. Write for narrow questions: one product, one comparison, one alternatives list, with current prices and dates.
  3. Check that your robots.txt doesn't block AI crawlers. Ours lets ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude and Google's crawlers in; we block only our outbound link folder and three data-collection bots (Amazonbot, Bytespider and CCBot).
  4. Re-check vendor terms. AI answers repeat what pages say, so errors spread.

FAQ

How often do AI assistants cite small review sites?

In our 30-day test, rarely on broad questions: our two sites were cited in 9 of about 3,000 answers from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode, and never named in the answer text.

What kind of page got cited?

Narrow pages: a single product review, a head-to-head comparison or an alternatives list with current prices. On 15 narrow questions matched to pages we already had, 8 of 75 answers cited us.

Which sources do AI assistants cite most for AI tool questions?

In our data, YouTube (28% of answers), Reddit (24%) and Zapier (21%). AI tool directories were named only a handful of times.
