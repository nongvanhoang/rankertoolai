Where AI Gets Its AI Tool Recommendations: 630 Answers Across 5 Models

By Hoang Nong · November 28, 2026 · Data collected October 28 to November 9, 2026. See our methodology.

Disclosure: the data comes from LLM Pulse, which we used on a free 30-day trial, and we are an LLM Pulse affiliate. LLM Pulse had no say in this report. Read our disclosure.

Short Answer

There is no single source to win. Google's AI products lean heavily on YouTube (cited in 71% of their answers). ChatGPT leans on a handful of small comparison sites. Reddit and Zapier show up almost everywhere. AI tool directories barely appear.

We asked 5 AI systems the same 42 questions about choosing AI tools, such as "best AI meeting notes app", on three dates, then counted which websites each answer cited. Our own site was cited in 2 of the 630 answers, so we report this as observers, not winners. For what happened to our own pages over a full month, see our 30-day tracking report.

  • YouTube is mostly a Google AI source. Of the 188 answers citing YouTube, 180 came from AI Overviews and AI Mode. ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini together cited it 8 times in 378 answers.
  • ChatGPT favored small comparison sites. iTechGuides, Dupple, Rankite and The AI Rankings (22.2%) are not household names, yet each appeared in a fifth or more of ChatGPT's answers. None came close to that on the other systems.
  • Zapier was the most consistent. It was in the top 10 on all five systems, cited in 16 to 39 answers on each.
  • Reddit was second on four of the five systems. Only ChatGPT left it out of its top three.

What one AI said about review sites

Asked where to find honest AI tool reviews, Google AI Mode warned that the space is "flooded with affiliate-driven 'top 10' listicles and incentivized feedback", and pointed people to Reddit, low-star G2 and Capterra reviews, newsletters and independent YouTube testers. That is one answer, not a trend. We publish affiliate reviews ourselves, so it is fair to include it.

What we would do with this

These numbers show what gets cited, not why, so treat these as reasonable bets rather than proven tactics.

  1. Decide which AI matters to you first. A plan for Google's AI answers looks different from a plan for ChatGPT. Check where your AI referral visits actually come from.
  2. For Google's AI answers, publish real demos on YouTube. It was cited in 65% to 78% of answers on the two Google systems.
  3. For ChatGPT, make comparison pages easy to read and keep them current. Put the answer first, use a table and show when you last checked.
  4. Answer real questions on Reddit without dropping links.
  5. Do not count on directory listings alone for AI visibility.

How we did this, and what it cannot tell you

  • Tool: LLM Pulse ran each question and recorded the answers and their cited sources.
  • Sample: 42 generic questions with no brand named, in US English, run on October 28, November 2 and November 9, 2026, on 5 systems: 630 answers.
  • Counting: an answer counts once for a site, however many times it links there.
  • "Cited" means listed as a source. LLM Pulse records some sources without a rank (background sources). We could not confirm whether our counts exclude them, so a cited site may not be linked in the visible answer.
  • We wrote the questions, and they lean toward the tools we cover. Different questions would give different results.
  • This is a snapshot. Three runs over two weeks cannot show a long-term trend.
  • Some domains are not ordinary publishers. google.com (53 answers) and chatgpt.com (34) are left out of the first table. Canva and HeyGen sell the tools being discussed, so they are not independent sources.
  • Correlation only. Being cited does not prove a site is accurate, or that it earns traffic.

FAQ

Which websites do AI assistants cite most for AI tool recommendations?

In our 630 answers, YouTube (30% of answers), Reddit (21%) and Zapier (21%). A single answer can cite several sites.

Does ChatGPT cite different sources than Google's AI?

Yes. Google AI Overviews and AI Mode cited YouTube in 65% to 78% of their answers. ChatGPT barely cited YouTube and instead leaned on small comparison sites such as iTechGuides (40% of its answers), Dupple (31%) and Rankite (29%).

Do AI tool directories get cited by AI assistants?

Rarely. G2 appeared in 28 of 630 answers; Futurepedia, There's An AI For That, Toolify and FutureTools together appeared in 15.
