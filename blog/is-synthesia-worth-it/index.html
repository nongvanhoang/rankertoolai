What Synthesia Actually Does

Synthesia's pitch is turning a script into a professional talking-head video without a camera, actors, or a studio. You pick an avatar, paste or type your script, and it renders a presenter video — and the same script can be rendered in 140+ languages.

Avatar realism is generally rated strong enough for training and explainer content, where viewers focus on the message; the most common tell is a limited range of hand and body gestures rather than face or lip-sync quality. We did run one small check ourselves: a single sentence typed into the free plan turned into a presenter video, shown in the 25-second clip on our Synthesia review.

How this verdict was put together: the official Synthesia documentation and pricing, user reviews, and one short free-plan test. RankerToolAI has not produced a full multi-video project with Synthesia.

The real value is editing text instead of reshooting: update the script and re-render, in any of 140+ languages.

Who Synthesia Is Worth It For

✓
Enterprise L&D and compliance teams — SCORM export drops directly into existing LMS platforms without rework.
✓
Multinational teams — rendering the same training in 140+ languages from one script is a real time and cost saver over dubbing.
✓
Teams producing frequent explainer/product videos — no camera, actors, or studio booking needed for each update.

Who Synthesia Might Disappoint

✗
Individual creators on a tight budget — HeyGen's lower entry price and flexible tiers fit solo use cases better.
✗
Content needing maximum expressiveness — HeyGen's avatars are marginally more expressive for creator-style content.

Synthesia vs HeyGen: Which Should You Pick?

Synthesia's Pro plan costs $89/month ($64/month billed yearly); HeyGen generally has a lower entry price and more flexible tiers. The decision comes down to use case, not just price.

HeyGen wins on: entry price and slightly more expressive avatars for individual creator or agency content.

Synthesia wins on: 140+ language coverage, SCORM/LMS export, and enterprise features like Personal Avatar — all of which matter more for corporate training at scale than for one-off creator videos.

Final verdict: Synthesia is worth it for enterprise training and multilingual video at scale. Create a free demo video on your actual use case first — training script, not a generic test — before committing to Starter or Creator.

FAQs

Is Synthesia worth it for corporate training?

Yes, especially for training at scale. 140+ language support and easy script edits make it a strong fit for onboarding and compliance video; SCORM export for LMS platforms is included on the Enterprise plan.

Are Synthesia's avatars realistic enough?

For corporate training and explainer content, yes — avatar realism is strong enough that viewers focus on the message, not the artifice, though HeyGen's avatars are marginally more expressive for individual creator content.

Is Synthesia worth it vs HeyGen?

For enterprise/B2B training, Synthesia's 140+ languages and Enterprise features like SCORM export and SSO justify its price. For individual creators, HeyGen is often the more accessible pick.

Does Synthesia have a free trial?

Yes. The free Basic plan gives about 10 minutes of video a month with a watermark — enough to test avatar realism and the editing workflow before paying.

Synthesia Pricing Recap

The free Basic plan includes about 10 minutes of video a month (watermarked). Starter costs $29/month, or $18/month billed yearly. Pro costs $89/month, or $64/month billed yearly. Enterprise is custom-priced and adds SCORM export, SSO and live collaboration. Check synthesia.io/pricing for current limits.
