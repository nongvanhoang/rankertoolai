Which One Should You Pick?

Pictory: the most direct route from an article

Pictory lists "generate video from script or URL" on its free trial and paid plans. Its trial gives you 300 credits, covering up to 10 minutes and 3 videos, with each video capped at 5 minutes. That's enough to try your own article. The catch is usage: the $29 Starter plan includes 30 minutes a month, less than Fliki's entry plan lists. Read our Pictory review.

Fliki: more listed minutes for regular publishing

Fliki's Standard plan lists 180 minutes of credits at $28/mo, removes the watermark, and supports videos up to 15 minutes. On those numbers, it looks like the better deal if you publish often. Blog-to-video is limited on the free plan, so test with a short article first.

Lumen5: the most usable free plan for short clips

Lumen5's free plan allows 5 videos a month, up to 2 minutes each at 720p, with a Lumen5 outro. It can start from a link, script or document. It suits short social clips more than full YouTube videos, since the outro and 720p limit will show on anything more polished.

The Basic Workflow

  1. Choose an article with clear structure. Lists and clear headings translate into scenes better than dense prose.
  2. Paste the link or text into the tool and let it build a first script.
  3. Cut the script hard. A 1,500-word article doesn't need to become a 1,500-word video, so keep the strongest points.
  4. Review the matched visuals and captions scene by scene.
  5. Add a voiceover, then export in the shape your platform needs, such as vertical for Shorts or wide for YouTube.

What to Fix After Generation

  • Mismatched visuals: automatic stock matching works from the words, so it can miss the meaning.
  • Lines that read well but sound stiff: written sentences often need shortening to work when spoken.
  • Facts and names: check numbers, product names and captions, because errors are harder to fix after you publish a video.
  • Pacing: split dense scenes so captions aren't rushed.

For opinion-led content, your own voice usually builds more trust than an AI voiceover. AI voices fit straightforward explainers better.

FAQs

What is the best AI tool to turn blog posts into videos?

For most people, start with Pictory: generating video from a script or URL is included on every plan and in its free trial. Choose Fliki if you want more listed minutes for the money, or Lumen5 if you want a free plan for short, simple videos.

Can I turn a blog post into a video for free?

Yes, with limits. Lumen5's free plan allows 5 videos a month up to 2 minutes at 720p with a Lumen5 outro. Fliki's free plan includes limited blog-to-video use with a watermark. Pictory's 14-day trial covers 3 videos and up to 10 minutes.

How long does it take to make a video from a blog post?

We haven't run a timed test, so we won't promise a number. Generating a first draft is the quick part. Expect to spend most of your time correcting visuals, trimming the script and checking facts.
