How to Make Product Demo & Tutorial Videos Without Filming

Screen recording + AI presenter = a demo video you can update the day your UI changes, without booking a camera or re-recording your voice.

TL;DR: Record your screen once, write a short script, and let a Synthesia avatar present it. Re-render when features change. Starter fits a handful of help-center videos; Pro fits teams shipping new demos every month.

Product videos go stale fast. Every redesign or new feature makes last quarter's demo slightly wrong, and re-recording a founder or support lead on camera is the step everyone postpones. Separating the presenter from the product footage fixes that: the screen recording shows the product, an AI presenter explains it, and either half can be swapped independently.

Four Kinds of Product Video Worth Making

  • Homepage explainer (60–90 seconds): the problem, the product, one key result.
  • Feature walkthroughs (1–3 minutes): one video per feature, linked from release notes and in-app tips.
  • Help-center tutorials: answers to your top 10 support tickets, as video.
  • Sales follow-ups: a short tailored demo a rep can send after a call.

The Workflow

1. Record clean screen footage

Use any screen recorder (Loom, OBS, or your OS's built-in tool). Record at full resolution, hide personal data, and move the cursor slowly. You don't need to talk while recording.

2. Write a short script per scene

One idea per scene: what the viewer is looking at, why it matters, what to click next. Keep sentences short; product names should be spelled the way they're pronounced, or fixed with Synthesia's pronunciation settings.

3. Assemble in Synthesia

  • Pick a product-demo or explainer template.
  • Upload the screen recording as the scene background or a picture-in-picture element, with the avatar in a corner.
  • Add captions — many viewers watch help videos muted.

4. Publish and reuse

Download MP4s on Starter or above and embed them in your help center, onboarding emails and YouTube. On Pro, branded share pages and interactive videos let viewers click through to the next step.

5. Keep it current

When the UI changes, re-record only the affected clip and re-render. The script and presenter stay the same.

Good to know: Synthesia's free Basic plan lets you build a demo with watermarks to test the workflow. You'll need Starter or higher to download MP4s without the Synthesia logo.

Tips That Make Demos Convert

  • Show the result in the first 10 seconds, then explain how to get there.
  • One feature per video. Link them together instead of making one long tour.
  • End with one clear next step: start a trial, book a call, or open the feature.
  • Disclose that the presenter is an AI avatar if your audience would care.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I put my own screen recording behind a Synthesia avatar?

Yes. You can upload your own video or screenshots as scene media and place the avatar alongside them, which is the usual setup for software demos.

Do I need to show my face?

No. You can use a stock avatar, or create a personal avatar of yourself (Starter includes 3 personal avatars) if you want viewers to see you without filming each video.

Is an AI demo video good enough for a homepage?

For explainers and walkthroughs, usually yes, especially with real product footage carrying most of the screen. For brand films and emotional storytelling, filmed video is still stronger.
