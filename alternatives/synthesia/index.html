HeyGen — Best Synthesia Alternative for Marketing Video

★★★★★
9.0/10 Top Pick

HeyGen is the closest direct alternative to Synthesia: another AI avatar video tool with a free plan and a $29/month entry plan. The difference is how you pay. HeyGen's Creator plan ($29/month, about $24 billed yearly) gives 600 credits a month with videos up to 30 minutes, 1080p export and voice cloning. Pro ($49/month, about $41 yearly) adds 1,000 credits and 4K export. Its free plan gives 3 videos a month, each up to 1 minute.

HeyGen is aimed more at marketing, social and creator video, while Synthesia is aimed at training and company video. Synthesia keeps the edge where training teams care most: a clear monthly minute allowance, interactive videos on Pro, and SCORM export for LMS platforms on Enterprise. If you make marketing content, HeyGen is the natural first stop; if you make training content, compare both free plans before switching. See our full HeyGen vs Synthesia comparison.

❌ Cons vs Synthesia

What is the best Synthesia alternative?
