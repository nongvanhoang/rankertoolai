Merlin AI Alternatives (2026): Monica, Sider, HARPA and More

Short Answer

Most people comparing Merlin look at Monica, Sider and HARPA AI. Monica is the cheapest yearly multi-model plan ($99 a year for Pro). HARPA is free if you already pay for ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, and it is built for web automation. Sider has turned into an AI browser agent with its own cloud computer, from $10 a month. Merlin stays the better pick if you want a generous free tier (102 queries a day) and many models in one chat for $19 a month billed yearly.

Monica: the Cheapest Yearly Multi-Model Plan

Monica is the closest match to Merlin: a browser extension and web app that puts models from OpenAI, Anthropic and Google in one place, plus image and video generation. Its pricing page shows these yearly prices:

  • Free: 40 uses of basic models a day, with limited trials of image and video generation, ChatPDF and its AI agent.
  • Pro, $99 a year: 5,000 basic-model queries and 200 advanced-model queries a month, plus 1,500 Advanced Credits.
  • Max, $199 a year: unlimited basic and advanced models plus 4,500 Advanced Credits (shown with $299 crossed out).
  • Ultra, $995 a year: unlimited models plus 20,000 Advanced Credits.

Monica Pro costs less than half of Merlin Pro billed yearly ($99 against $228). The catch is the cap of 200 advanced-model queries a month on Pro. If you use the strongest models every day, compare Monica Max at $199 a year with Merlin Pro instead.

Sider: an AI Browser Agent, Not Just a Chat Sidebar

Sider started as a ChatGPT sidebar, but its current plans are built around "Sider Hand", an agent that automates tasks on real websites: comparing prices, filling in forms, researching across sites and triaging a Gmail inbox. Every plan includes chat with GPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek and Grok, image and video generation, PDF chat and translation.

Monthly prices on its pricing page are Nano $10 ($9 for the first month), Lite $25, Pro $50 and Max $100. Plans differ in monthly credits and in the cloud computer behind the agent: Nano gets temporary access on demand, while Lite and above get a dedicated cloud computer that runs around the clock. Yearly billing is advertised at up to 25% off plus bonus credits.

Choose Sider if you want an agent to do browser chores for you. If you mainly chat with several models, Merlin and Monica cost less for that job.

HARPA AI: Free on Your Own AI Login

HARPA works differently. Its free plan runs on the ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini login you already have: 100 chat messages a day, 1,000 page-aware runs in total, 3 custom commands and 3 automations, with no card needed. It also supports chats with your own API key.

Pro is $144 a year ($12 a month) and adds unlimited runs and automations plus about 2,300 cloud requests a year on top models. Teams starts at $12 per seat a month. X Lifetime is a one-time $240 with about 900 cloud requests and 40% off every top-up.

Two things to know before paying: HARPA's pricing page says paid plans run to the end of the term and are not refunded, and its strength is automation (watching pages, running commands on what you are reading), not a big chat allowance. If you already pay $20 for ChatGPT or Claude, HARPA's free plan may be all you need on top.

ChatGPT Plus and Claude Pro

If you mostly use one provider, its own plan is the simplest choice. ChatGPT Plus and Claude Pro are $20 a month each and get each provider's newest features first. You lose the ability to switch models mid-conversation, which is the main reason people pick Merlin, Monica or Sider.

When to Stay with Merlin

  • You want a real free tier. Merlin's free plan allows 102 queries a day, against Monica's 40 basic-model uses.
  • You switch models often. Merlin has models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, DeepSeek and Mistral in one chat, plus page, PDF and YouTube summaries in its extension.
  • You want to try a paid month. Merlin Pro is $29 month to month, so you can test it before committing to a year.
  • A team needs it. Merlin Teams is $15 per seat a month billed yearly, with a 2-seat minimum.

FAQ

What is the best free alternative to Merlin AI?

If you already pay for ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, HARPA AI's free plan runs on that login with 100 chats a day. Without a paid AI account, Merlin's own free plan (102 queries a day) is more generous than Monica's (40 basic-model uses a day).

Is Monica cheaper than Merlin?

Yes, billed yearly. Monica Pro is $99 a year against $228 for Merlin Pro, but Monica Pro caps advanced-model use at 200 queries a month. Monica Max ($199 a year) removes that cap.

Is Sider still a ChatGPT sidebar?

Sider still includes chat with GPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek and Grok, but its plans are now built around Sider Hand, an AI agent that runs browser tasks on a cloud computer. Plans start at $10 a month.

Can I use my own API key instead of a subscription?

HARPA AI supports chats on your own API key and on your existing ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini login. Merlin, Monica and Sider sell their own usage plans.

Do these tools offer refunds?

HARPA's pricing page says paid plans are not refunded. Monica's and Sider's pricing pages don't state a refund policy, so check their terms before paying for a year.

Which Merlin alternative is best for teams?

Merlin Teams is $15 per seat a month billed yearly (2-seat minimum) and HARPA Teams starts at $12 per seat a month. Both add shared workspaces; compare what each counts as usage before you buy seats.
