How to Use It

  1. Export each company's trial balance for the same period end from Xero, QuickBooks, Sage, MYOB or your ledger.
  2. Paste each one into a company sheet and map every account to a group line.
  3. Enter the intercompany eliminations: sales and costs between group companies, and the balances they owe each other.
  4. Check every "Check" cell says OK.
  5. Copy the consolidated P&L and balance sheet into your monthly pack.

Step-by-step guides for each ledger: Xero, Sage and MYOB.

When to Move Beyond a Spreadsheet

A spreadsheet is fine for two or three simple companies. It starts costing more time than it saves when:

  • you consolidate every month and re-export each trial balance by hand,
  • a subsidiary reports in another currency,
  • new accounts keep breaking the mapping, or
  • a board, bank or investor wants the same pack on a fixed date.

At that point a consolidation app connects to each ledger and refreshes automatically. Joiin starts at $23/mo for one company billed annually ($29/mo for two), with eliminations on every plan. FAQ

Is the template really free?

Yes. You can download it directly without an email. If you enter your email we'll also send the link to your inbox and a few short emails on consolidation; unsubscribe anytime.

Which accounting software does it work with?

Any that can export a trial balance to Excel or CSV, including Xero, QuickBooks, Sage, MYOB and FreeAgent. You paste each company's trial balance into its own sheet.

How many companies does it handle?

Three company sheets are included. To add more, copy a company sheet and add a column for it on the Consolidated TB sheet.

Does it handle foreign currency?

No. Translate each subsidiary's trial balance into the group currency before you paste it. Consolidation software such as Joiin (Pro and Max plans) or Fathom Pro handles multi-currency automatically.

What are the eliminations for?

When group companies trade with each other, those sales, costs and balances would be double-counted. The Eliminations sheet removes them, with two lines per elimination that cancel out.
