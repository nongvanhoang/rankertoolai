Switch from Granola to Wispr Flow: Up to 12 Months Free

Updated October 17, 2026 · 4 min read · RankerToolAI editorial team

If you pay for Granola, the AI meeting-notes app, Wispr Flow is running a limited-time offer to win you over: it will cover the rest of your paid Granola plan and add 3 bonus months. That is up to 12 months of Wispr Flow's Notetaker and dictation without paying.

Below is how the offer works, who it is for, and what you actually get with Wispr Flow compared with Granola, so you can decide whether the switch makes sense for you.

The offer at a glance

  • Who: anyone currently paying for Granola
  • What you get: 3 free months of Wispr Flow right away, then the remaining time on your Granola plan covered
  • Maximum: 12 months of Notetaker + dictation free
  • Limit: one per customer
  • Deadline: limited-time; Wispr Flow has not published an end date

How the switch works

  1. Open the offer link and enter your email. Your 3 free months of Wispr Flow start straight away.
  2. Send Wispr Flow your Granola details (the plan you are paying for) when they ask, so they can confirm the time left on it.
  3. Wispr Flow covers the rest of your Granola plan on top of the 3 months, up to 12 months in total.

Keep a copy of your Granola invoice or billing page handy: it is the simplest proof of what plan you are on and when it renews.

Granola vs Wispr Flow: what changes when you switch

The two products overlap on meeting notes but are not identical. Granola is built around meetings. Wispr Flow started as a voice-dictation app and added a Notetaker, so one subscription covers both.

Who should take the offer

  • You already type a lot (emails, Slack, docs). Dictation is the part Granola does not do, and it comes in the same subscription.
  • You want to cut one subscription and are happy with transcript + summary + next steps for meetings.
  • You have months left on a Granola plan, so the buyout is worth more to you.

Who might stay on Granola

  • Teams that rely on Granola's shared folders, Business features (follow-up emails, pre-meeting briefs, API) or Enterprise controls like SSO and HIPAA workspaces.
  • Anyone whose meeting workflow is built around Granola's note templates and Recipes.

Because the first 3 months are free either way, the low-risk approach is to claim the offer, run Wispr Flow's Notetaker on your next few meetings, and only then decide about Granola.

FAQ

Who qualifies for the Granola switch offer?
Wispr Flow says the offer is for anyone currently paying for Granola. You sign up through the offer link, then send Wispr Flow your Granola plan details so they can confirm how much time is left on it.
How much free time do I get?
You get 3 free months as soon as you enter your email. Wispr Flow then covers the remaining time on your paid Granola plan. The total is capped at 12 months.
Do I have to cancel Granola?
Wispr Flow covers the rest of your Granola plan so you are not paying twice. Whether and when you cancel Granola is up to you; check Granola's own billing settings for how cancellation works on your plan.
Does the free period include the Notetaker?
Yes. Wispr Flow sells one subscription that includes both Flow dictation and Notetaker, and the offer covers Notetaker and dictation.
I'm on Granola's free Basic plan. Does this apply?
The offer is described as being for people paying for Granola, so free Basic users should not expect the plan buyout. Wispr Flow's own free plan and free trial are still available.
When does the offer end?
Wispr Flow calls it a limited-time offer and has not published an end date. It can end at any time.
